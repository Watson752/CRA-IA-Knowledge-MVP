# Public CRA audit and evaluation case studies

This folder contains learning notes that summarize **publicly released** CRA internal audit and evaluation reports. Each case separates **what the published report states** from this vault’s clearly labelled **cross-domain interpretation**. Historical findings are not statements about current CRA conditions unless a newer official source says so. See [[Interpreting Historical Public Audit Findings]]. Each case should include a **Follow-up evidence in vault** section stating current remediation status is unknown unless a follow-up source is linked.

Master index with demo journeys: [[00-Start/Public-Audit-Case-Library]].

## Journey 1 — Governance, technology, and control design

Start with [[Internal Audit - Oversight Use and Continuous Improvement of Business Intelligence]] to distinguish data/tool governance from the governance of BI products and their lifecycle. Continue to [[Internal Audit - Specific Cyber Security Controls]] and [[Internal Audit - Enterprise Fraud Management System]] for policy alignment, monitoring technology, and report redaction limits.

Useful concepts: [[Business Intelligence]], [[Data Governance]], [[Cybersecurity]], [[Defence in Depth]], [[Three Lines Model]], [[Evidence Reliability]].

## Journey 2 — Operational decisions and fairness

Read [[Internal Audit - Charities Audit Process]] for information gathering, risk assessment, documented approvals, and monitoring supporting consistent decisions. Then [[Internal Audit - Accounts Receivable National Inventory]] for national collections/inventory, risk scoring, local practices, and management information.

Useful concepts: [[Population Completeness]], [[Missing Data]], [[Data Quality]], [[Evidence Reliability]], [[Performance Reporting]], [[Business Intelligence]].

## Journey 3 — Reporting, statistics, and outcomes

Begin with [[Evaluation - Audit Yield]] (public reporting/results-information demo). It contrasts fiscal impact and cash-recovery outcomes, documents population and sample methods, and explains cross-system matching barriers. Follow with [[Internal Audit - Accounts Receivable National Inventory]] for metric definition, attribution, timeliness, and inventory movement.

**Disclaimer (vault research 2026-07-23):** *Internal Audit – Tax and Benefits Operations Results Information* was referenced as underway in **2018** public material; a **final published report was not located on Canada.ca**. Journey 3 uses Evaluation – Audit Yield instead.

Useful concepts: [[Performance Reporting]], [[Population Completeness]], [[Missing Data]], [[Data Quality]], [[Evidence Reliability]], [[Business Intelligence]], [[Data Governance]], [[How Statistical Limitations Affect Audit Conclusions]], [[How Missing Data Limits Audit Assurance]].

## Source practice

Open each note’s official Canada.ca URL (see [[99-Sources/CRA-Public-Source-Register]] and matching `SRC-*` source notes) before relying on a claim. Treat redacted material and out-of-scope subjects as unknown. Treat management action plans as intended responses, not evidence of completion.
