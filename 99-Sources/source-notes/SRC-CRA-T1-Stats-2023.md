# SRC: CRA T1 Final Statistics (2023 tax year)

## What this source is

CRA **Individual Income Tax Return Statistics** for the **2023 tax year** (2025 edition page), including tables, metadata, and **public data confidentiality procedures** for aggregate T1 statistics.

## Key claims extractable (public)

**Confidentiality and presentation**

- Data **suppressed where warranted** to protect tax filer information.
- Counts **rounded to nearest multiple of 10** (examples: 104 → 100; 105 → 110).
- Dollar amounts in **Tables 1–5 rounded to nearest thousand**.
- **Totals may not add** due to rounding or suppression.
- **Zero [0]** indicates suppressed information; suppression **includes valid zeroes**.

**Coverage and timing**

- **Assessment cut-off: November 30, 2024** (earlier extraction versus **June 30, 2025** referenced for the prior approach) to improve timeliness; comparability caution when contrasting editions.
- Statistics reflect the **most recent assessment or reassessment** available at cut-off.
- Data are **subject to revision**.

**Geography and tables**

- Table 1 uses **province or territory of taxation**; Tables 2–5 use **province or territory of residence** (definitions on page).

## Reliability notes

- Authoritative for published aggregate statistics and disclosure rules for this edition.
- Not microdata; suppressed cells cannot be inferred from published tables.
- Tax-year statistics differ in period and definition from fiscal-year departmental indicators ([[99-Sources/source-notes/SRC-CRA-DP-2026-27]], [[99-Sources/source-notes/SRC-CRA-DRR-2024-25]]).
