# SRC: CRA Internal Audit and Program Evaluation

## What this source is

The **Internal Audit and Program Evaluation** landing page on Canada.ca is CRA’s public index to published internal audit and evaluation reports, with descriptive text on the function’s purpose and accountability posting practices.

## Key claims extractable (public)

- IA/PE supports strategic goals with independent, objective information, advice, and assurance; completed reports are posted for **public accountability** (as stated on the landing page).
- Audits are described as **risk-based**, focusing on risk management, management control framework, and information for decision-making and reporting (wording on the landing page as verified 2026-07-23).
- Links to report series by year, including internal audits and evaluations referenced in this vault’s case library.
