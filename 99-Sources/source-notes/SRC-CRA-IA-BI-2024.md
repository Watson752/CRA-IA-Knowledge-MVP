# SRC: Internal Audit – Business Intelligence (2024)

## What this source is

Final internal audit report **Internal Audit – Oversight, Use, and Continuous Improvement of Business Intelligence**, presented to the Audit Committee **18 June 2024** (Canada.ca page details **2024-10-15**).

## Key claims extractable (public)

- Audit period **1 April 2020 – 31 March 2023**; examination **June 2023 – November 2023**.
- Objective: assurance that BI is overseen, used, and continuously improved for compliance, collections, and verification programs.
- Published findings on governance framework, horizontal coordination, continuous improvement, and tool deployment; four recommendations with SIIB-led management action plans and target dates through **June 2025** (as stated in the report).

## Reliability notes

- Authoritative for report-stated scope, methodology summary, findings, recommendations, and management responses.
- Historical to the audit period; do not infer current control state without a newer official source.
- Out-of-scope items (data security, HR, infrastructure lifecycle) are explicitly excluded in the report.
