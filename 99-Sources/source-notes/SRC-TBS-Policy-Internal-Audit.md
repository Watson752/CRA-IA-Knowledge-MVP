# SRC: TBS Policy on Internal Audit

## What this source is

Treasury Board **Policy on Internal Audit**, effective **June 15, 2023**, replacing the April 1, 2017 policy. Sets government-wide requirements for internal audit functions, chief audit executives, departmental audit committees, and Comptroller General oversight.

## Key claims extractable (public)

- Expected results include deputy heads supported as accounting officers by IA contributing to risk management, control, and governance; independent advice from departmental audit committees; and professional IA community capacity overseen by the Comptroller General.
- Departments with reference level **$300 million or more** per year must have an internal audit function; IA carried out per **IIA International Professional Practices Framework** unless conflict with policy (policy prevails).
- Deputy heads must ensure **formal responses** to IA recommendations, **public release** of completed reports on prescribed platforms within Comptroller General timeframes, and appropriate chief audit executive independence (direct report to deputy head, unrestricted access to records and audit committee).
- **Departmental audit committees** provide objective advice on sufficiency, quality, and results of IA engagements related to risk management, control, and governance frameworks.
