# SRC: Internal Audit – Charities Audit Process (2025)

## What this source is

Internal audit report on the **Charities Directorate** audit process, report date **14 January 2025**, audit period **1 April 2020 – 31 March 2023**, examination **June 2023 – January 2024**.

## Key claims extractable (public)

- Objective: assurance that key audit processes support impartial conduct of charities audits.
- Published counts: approximately **86,000** registered charities; **545** audits completed **2020–21 through 2022–23** (541 Compliance; 4 RAD) as stated in the report.
- Findings on risk indicators, documentation, monitoring, and impartiality; recommendations to LPRAB with action-plan targets through **March 2026**.

## Reliability notes

- Authoritative for report-stated process descriptions and findings in the public HTML/PDF.
- Excluded scope (broader effectiveness, certain national-security activities) limits generalization beyond charities audit process controls.
