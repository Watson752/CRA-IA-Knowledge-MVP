# SRC: CRA Organization (Ministerial Transition 2025)

## What this source is

The **Organization** page under CRA’s **Ministerial Transition 2025** microsite is a public overview of CRA’s mandate, governance roles, headquarters branch mandates, Corporate Secretariat, regions/office types, and high-level organizational structure.

## Organizational period described

Ministerial Transition **2025** briefing content. Canada.ca page details date observed: **2025-09-09**. Vault accessed **2026-07-25**.

## Key claims extractable (public)

- Roles of the Minister of National Revenue, Board of Management, Commissioner and CEO, Deputy Commissioner, and Taxpayers’ Ombudsperson.
- CRA comprises **14 headquarters branches**, a **Corporate Secretariat**, and **4 regions**.
- Six program HQ branches; eight corporate HQ branches; regions deliver via field offices.
- Public mandates for each named headquarters branch and geographic scope for regions.
- Legend for AC, DAC, RAC, ADM, ED/SGC.

## Reliability notes

- Authoritative for publicly described governance roles, branch list/mandates, Corporate Secretariat, regions/office types, and AC/DAC/RAC/ADM/ED/SGC legend as of the 2025 transition package.
- Not a detailed org chart below branch level; does not list all subdivisions or internal reporting lines.
- **Vault rule:** treat the organizational chart’s named individuals as a **historical public snapshot**. Do not treat Bob Hamilton or other chart names as current unless a newer official page confirms them.
