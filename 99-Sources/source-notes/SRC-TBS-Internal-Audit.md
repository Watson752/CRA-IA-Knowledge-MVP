# SRC: Treasury Board Internal Audit (Policy and community)

## What this source is

Treasury Board Secretariat **Internal audit** organizational page describing the Internal Audit Sector’s role, the government-wide **Policy on Internal Audit**, and the **Directive on Internal Audit**, plus community and professional resources.

## Key claims extractable (public)

- Internal audit provides deputy heads assurance on governance, risk management, and control processes; supports value and accountability to Canadians.
- Internal Audit Sector (Office of the Comptroller General) is responsible for the **Policy on Internal Audit** and health of the federal internal audit community.
- Policy objectives include reinforcing **independence and objectivity**, increasing qualifications of public sector internal auditors, and clarifying responsibilities between the Comptroller General and deputy heads.
- **Directive on Internal Audit** sets mandatory attributes for departmental audit committees and procedures for internal auditing in the Government of Canada, including public reporting on internal audit performance results.
- Sector responsibilities include policy/professional practices, community development, and Audit Operations engagements.
