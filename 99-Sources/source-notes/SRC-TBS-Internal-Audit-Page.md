# SRC: TBS Internal Audit (landing page)

## What this source is

Treasury Board Secretariat **Internal audit** page describing the government-wide internal audit function, the Internal Audit Sector of the Office of the Comptroller General, and links to the **Policy on Internal Audit** and community resources.

## Key claims extractable (public)

- Internal audit provides feedback on management practices and assists deputy heads with assurance on governance, risk management, and control processes.
- Internal Audit Sector responsibilities include **policy and professional practices**, **community development**, and **audit operations** (horizontal and departmental engagements as described on the page).
- Policy on Internal Audit is described as reinforcing independence, qualifications, and division of responsibilities between the Comptroller General and deputy heads.
