# SRC: CRA Departmental Results Report 2024–25

## What this source is

The **2024–25 Departmental Results Report** (DRR) is CRA’s account of **actual** results achieved against the prior plan’s indicators for fiscal year **2024–25**, including financial and non-financial performance information.

## Key claims extractable (public)

- **Actual** core responsibility spending for **2024–25**: tax **$4,767,625,053**; benefits **$16,182,472,509** (label **Actual** and fiscal year; benefits includes statutory payments per report footnotes).
- **Actual** results for indicators in the CRA Departmental Results Framework for **2024–25**.
- Narrative on service delivery, compliance, benefits administration, and modernization (as published).
- Financial statements and spending compared to plans (read footnotes for benefits vs tax administration comparability).

## Reliability notes

- Authoritative for **reported actuals** in the covered fiscal year.
- Not a substitute for tax-year assessment statistics ([[03-Statistics/Individual Income Tax Return Statistics]]); indicators may use different definitions and periods.
- Historical snapshot; control environment and metrics may change in later years.
