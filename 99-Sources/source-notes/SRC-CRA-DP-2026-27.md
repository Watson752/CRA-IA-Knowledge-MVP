# Source: Canada Revenue Agency’s 2026–27 Departmental Plan

Official CRA Departmental Plan describing priorities, plans, and associated costs for the upcoming three fiscal years.

## Key extractable claims (examples)

- Vision, mission, values
- Three strategic priorities (A integrity; B efficiency; C workforce) with objectives
- Core responsibilities: tax and benefits
- **Departmental Results Framework** with **12** departmental result indicators (**10** tax, **2** benefits)
- Priority **A1**: strengthen cyber and data security and fraud prevention
- Priority **B**: enhance operational efficiency (B1 quality services; B2 business processes; B3 digital tools and data-driven approaches)
- Priority **C**: high-performing workforce (C1 digital/data skills; C2 core values)

## Reliability notes

Primary official planning document. Distinguish **planned** figures from **actual** results in the Departmental Results Report.
