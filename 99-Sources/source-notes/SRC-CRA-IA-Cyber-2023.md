# SRC: Internal Audit – Cyber Security Controls (2023)

## What this source is

Internal audit report **Internal Audit – Specific Cyber Security Controls** (March **2023**), covering a subset of higher-risk cyber controls for the audited period **December 1, 2021 – August 31, 2022**.

## Key claims extractable (public)

- Objective: assurance that controls safeguard CRA IT from digital attack (subset of controls, not full posture).
- Published recommendations include Security Branch establishing a **second line of defence** for cyber security and updating corporate policy instruments to align with the Treasury Board **Policy on Service and Digital**; additional recommendation detail is protected in the published HTML.
- Management agreed; report describes first-line, planned second-line GRC, and third-line AERB roles at a high level.

## Reliability notes

- Authoritative only for non-protected content in the published report.
- Protected findings and recommendations must not be reconstructed from this vault.
- Historical snapshot; not evidence of current vulnerability or control effectiveness.
