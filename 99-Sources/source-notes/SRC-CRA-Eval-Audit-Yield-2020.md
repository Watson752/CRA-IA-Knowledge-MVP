# SRC: Evaluation – Audit Yield (2020)

## What this source is

Final evaluation report **Evaluation – Audit yield**, **January 2020**, assessing CRA’s ability to measure cash recovered from audit debit reassessments (income tax and GST/HST). Canada.ca page details **2020-02-14**.

## Key claims extractable (public)

- Defines **audit yield** as cash paid/collected attributable to audit activities; contrasts with **fiscal impact** (assessed tax, refunds reduced, interest/penalties, present value of future federal tax assessable; excludes appeals reversals and uncollected amounts).
- As of **July 2019**: estimated **60–64%** recovery on income-tax collectible cash from debit reassessments closed **FY 2016–17**; **71%** on GST/HST collectible cash from debit reassessments closed **FY 2015–16** (snapshots, not performance conclusions).
- Income tax fiscal impact **$9.6 billion** (FY **2016–17**); collectible cash portion **$6.4–6.8 billion** (67–71%); GST/HST fiscal impact **$2.60 billion** (FY **2015–16**); collectible **$1.77 billion** (68%).
- Two recommendations on integrated cash-collected measure and automated cross-system linking; management action targets **December 31, 2020** and **June 30, 2021**.

## Reliability notes

- Authoritative for definitions, methodology summary, and published estimates in the report tables.
- Snapshot timing (July 2019) and single-year cohorts limit trend or current-performance claims.
- T3 trust line noted **out of scope** for audit yield at time of report.
