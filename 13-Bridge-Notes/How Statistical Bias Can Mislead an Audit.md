## How bias enters

Incomplete [[Sampling Frame]]s, filtered extracts, convenience samples treated as random, analyzing only “successful” items, or omitting rejects/leavers ([[Missing Data]], [[Rejected Records]], [[Dormant Accounts]]).

## Effect on conclusions

Biased evidence can look precise yet fail [[Analytical Validity]]. Narrow [[Audit Conclusion]] wording; do not extrapolate from [[Judgmental Sampling]] as if random ([[How Statistical Limitations Affect Audit Conclusions]]).

## Inquiry discipline

Bias is a **risk indicator** for evidence quality—not itself a finding until tested against [[Criteria]].
