# How the CRA Business Intelligence Audit Supports the Knowledge-Vault Concept

> **Derived analysis:** This note is an interpretation by the MVP author. The published CRA internal audit did **not** recommend Obsidian, a knowledge graph, RAG, or this vault.

## What the published report states

The 2024 public report [[Internal Audit - Oversight Use and Continuous Improvement of Business Intelligence]] found (for the audit period 2020–21 to 2022–23) that important BI framework components existed, while improvements were needed around:

- CRA-wide BI objectives, roles, and a common definition
- horizontal coordination and leveraging of BI
- continuous improvement expectations
- timely deployment of BI tools

The published report states that without a method to document BI efforts, duplication risk rises and horizontal leverage is limited. It also notes limited joint BI projects and siloed work despite working groups.

## This vault interprets the issue as

A personal, public-sources-only knowledge vault can **illustrate**—for learning—how governed repositories, clear classification, and reusable concepts reduce fragmented knowledge. That illustration is pedagogical. It is not a claim that CRA should adopt this toolset, nor that the audit endorsed it.
