# Board of Management

## Stable role information

The Board of Management is the statutory governance body publicly described as overseeing the **organization and administration** of the Canada Revenue Agency.

Official CRA material states the Board:

- Supports world-class tax and benefit administration
- Develops/approves the Corporate Business Plan; approves the Departmental Results Report and annual audited financial statements
- Oversees organization and management of the CRA
- Has **no authority** in the administration or enforcement of program/tax legislation
- May not direct the Commissioner or an employee in statutory powers
- Is not authorized to receive personal/business information obtained under program legislation

Supported by the [[Corporate Secretariat]] / Board Secretariat. See [[CRA-Governance-Structure]].

## Composition (public description)

**Fifteen directors:**

| Seat | Count |
|------|-------|
| Chair | 1 |
| Federal representatives | 2 |
| Provincial representatives | 10 |
| Territorial representative | 1 |
| Commissioner (ex officio) | 1 |

The Board **oversees organization and administration**; the **Commissioner** is accountable to the Board for **daily management** ([[Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer]]).

## Position in the public governance structure

```
Parliament ← Minister of National Revenue (accountable for CRA)
Board of Management → organization, admin, Corporate Business Plan
Commissioner and CEO → day-to-day admin & enforcement (Minister's delegated authority)
```

Tax enforcement authority rests with the Commissioner under the Minister—not with the Board.
