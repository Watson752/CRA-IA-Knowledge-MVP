# Audit Committee

The Audit Committee is a committee of the CRA **Board of Management**. A **public charter** is published on Canada.ca.

## Public mandate or responsibility

From the source facts available for this vault note (high-level governance context, not full charter text):

- The **Board** oversees CRA organization and management and supports world-class tax and benefit administration ([[Board of Management]]).
- Board-level governance connects to **internal audit and program evaluation (IA/PE)** planning and reporting in the public governance story of the agency; [[Audit, Evaluation, and Risk Branch|AERB]] and the IA landing page describe IA/PE as supporting strategic goals with independent assurance and public posting of results. See [[CRA-Governance-Structure]].

**Limitation:** This note was built from organizational and IA landing summaries plus the charter **URL**. The detailed duties, membership rules, meeting cadence, and explicit charter obligations were **not** transcribed here. For authoritative committee terms, read the published charter directly.

## Position in the public governance structure

Committee of [[Board of Management]] → supports board oversight distinct from [[Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer]] operational management and distinct from tax law administration (which the Board does not perform).

## Related CRA priorities

- Priority **A** (integrity) and **B** (efficiency) benefit from IA/PE assurance described at the function level ([[CRA Strategic Priorities]])

## Related published audits or evaluations

- [[Internal Audit and Program Evaluation]] — audits conducted per **International Standards for the Professional Practice of Internal Auditing** (IA landing page)

## Related business processes

- Risk-based internal audit focusing on risk management, management control framework, and information for decision-making and reporting (IA landing page)
