# Taxpayers Ombudsperson

## Stable role information

Official CRA Organization page (Agency Governance Overview / Taxpayers’ Ombudsperson sections) describes the role as:

- Operating at **arm’s length** from the CRA
- **Accountable directly to the Minister** ([[Minister of National Revenue]])
- Having **no authority** in the administration or enforcement of legislation for which the CRA remains fully accountable to the Minister
- Mandated to improve service via complaint mechanisms, systemic reporting, and outreach
- Upholding the eight service-related rights in the Taxpayer Bill of Rights

**Official organizational relationship:** Ombudsperson Liaison Office (OLO) is located in [[Service, Innovation, and Integration Branch|SIIB]]; the SIIB Assistant Commissioner, as Chief Service Officer, holds monthly bilateral meetings with the Ombudsperson.

## Position in the public governance structure

Separate from [[Board of Management]] (organization/admin oversight) and from [[Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer]] day-to-day operations. See [[CRA-Governance-Structure]].
