# CRA Regions

The CRA organizes **four regions** for **program delivery**, alongside fourteen headquarters branches ([[CRA Headquarters Branches]]).

> Expanded region notes: [[CRA-Regions]] · [[Atlantic Region]] · [[Quebec Region]] · [[Ontario Region]] · [[Western Region]].

## Public mandate or responsibility

Regions deliver CRA **program** services geographically. The ministerial transition organization page names the four regions:

1. [[Atlantic Region|Atlantic]]
2. [[Quebec Region|Quebec]]
3. [[Ontario Region|Ontario]]
4. [[Western Region|Western]]

The same official page enumerates public office types used in regional delivery (TSOs, Tax Centres, NVCCs, Contact Centres, Northern Service Centres, Centres of Expertise). It does not publish staffing structures or complete office inventories.

## Position in the public governance structure

Regions complement HQ **program** branches (Appeals; Assessment, Benefit, and Service; Collections and Verification; Compliance Programs; Legislative Policy and Regulatory Affairs; Service, Innovation, and Integration). Corporate HQ branches (IT, Security, Finance, etc.) support enterprise-wide capabilities. Exact headings: [[CRA-Program-Branches]].

Executive accountability remains with [[Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer]] under [[Minister of National Revenue]] delegated authority.
