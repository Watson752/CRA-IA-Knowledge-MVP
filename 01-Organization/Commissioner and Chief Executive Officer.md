# Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer

## Stable role information

Official sources describe the Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer as:

- responsible for the **day-to-day management and direction** of the CRA
- accountable to the Minister, the Board, and the Clerk of the Privy Council
- responsible for day-to-day administration and enforcement under the Minister’s delegated authority
- **ex officio** member of the [[Board of Management]]
- advising the Minister on legislated authorities, duties, and functions

Supported by the [[Deputy Commissioner]]. See [[CRA-Governance-Structure]].

> Individuals named on the 2025 organizational chart are a **historical public snapshot**, not automatic proof of current incumbency.
