# Deputy Commissioner

## Stable role information

Official organization material states that the Deputy Commissioner:

- supports the Commissioner in all duties
- exercises specific duties/functions assigned by the Commissioner
- acts for the Commissioner in case of absence, incapacity, or vacancy
- is not a member of the Board of Management but normally attends Board meetings

See [[Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer]] · [[CRA-Governance-Structure]].
