# Minister of National Revenue

## Stable role information

Official CRA organizational material states that the Minister of National Revenue is:

- accountable to Parliament and the Prime Minister for CRA operations and matters relating to collection of taxes and duties under named statutes, and elements of EI and CPP legislation
- holder of powers related to regulation-making and reporting to Parliament and the Governor in Council
- **not** directing officials on interpreting tax law
- a member of Cabinet and Privy Councillor

The Commissioner exercises day-to-day administration and enforcement under delegated authority from the Minister ([[Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer]]). The [[Taxpayers Ombudsperson]] is accountable to the Minister while operating at arm’s length.

See [[CRA-Governance-Structure]].

## Position in the public governance structure

```
Parliament
    └── Minister of National Revenue (accountable for CRA)
            ├── Delegated authority → Commissioner and CEO (operations)
            └── Accountability relationship → Taxpayers' Ombudsperson
Board of Management → oversees CRA organization/admin (not tax enforcement)
```

The Board of Management does **not** replace ministerial accountability to Parliament and does **not** administer or enforce tax legislation ([[Board of Management]]).
