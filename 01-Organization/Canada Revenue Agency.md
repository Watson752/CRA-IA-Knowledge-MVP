# Canada Revenue Agency

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) is the federal organization publicly described as administering tax, benefits, and related programs on behalf of governments across Canada. Public planning documents state a vision of trusted, fair, and helpful tax and benefits administration that puts people first, and a mission to ensure compliance while contributing to Canadians’ economic and social well-being.

## Public mandate or responsibility

CRA’s published mission is to administer tax, benefits, and related programs and to ensure compliance on behalf of governments across Canada, thereby contributing to the ongoing economic and social well-being of Canadians. Two core responsibilities are publicly identified: **Tax** and **Benefits** (see [[CRA Core Responsibilities]] and [[CRA Mandate]]).

## Position in the public governance structure

- The **Minister of National Revenue** is accountable to Parliament for the CRA ([[Minister of National Revenue]]).
- The **Board of Management** oversees the organization and administration of the CRA, including development of the Corporate Business Plan, but has **no authority** over the administration or enforcement of tax legislation ([[Board of Management]]).
- The **Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer** is responsible for day-to-day administration and enforcement under the Minister’s delegated authority and is accountable to the Board for daily management ([[Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer]]).
- The **Taxpayers’ Ombudsperson** operates at arm’s length and is accountable to the Minister ([[Taxpayers Ombudsperson]]).

Organizationally, the CRA comprises **14 headquarters branches**, a [[Corporate Secretariat]], and **4 regions**: six program branches at headquarters, eight corporate branches, and four regions for program delivery ([[CRA Headquarters Branches]], [[CRA-Regions]], [[CRA-Organizational-Overview]]).

## Known limitations of the public description

- Public pages do not provide a full internal org chart, reporting lines below named executives, or complete branch subdivisions.
- Planned spending and FTE figures for **2026–27** are **planned** figures from the departmental plan and must not be read as actual results (contrast with **actual** 2024–25 results in the departmental results report where labeled).
- Benefits **spending** in results reports includes statutory benefit payments and is not comparable to tax administration spending without reading footnotes and labels.
