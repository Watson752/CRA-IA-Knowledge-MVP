# Test-06: Independence and Historical Findings (Post-Fix)

## Question

Who is responsible for managing risks and correcting audit issues, what is Internal Audit responsible for, and how should a historical public audit finding be interpreted today?

## Post-fix answer (vault-supported)

[[Ownership and Assurance Roles]], [[Internal Audit Independence]], [[Three Lines Model]], [[Audit Client]], [[Control Ownership]] (alias Control Owner), [[Business Process Owner]], and [[Management Action Plan Owner]] separate management accountability from AERB assurance. AERB note includes an onboarding clarification that ERM advice ≠ first-line ownership or MAP execution.

[[Interpreting Historical Public Audit Findings]] plus case **Historical context** banners and **Follow-up evidence in vault = unknown** standardize temporal reading. [[Follow-up]] states implementation verification ≠ automatic OE and does not transfer control ownership to IA.

Example: BI tool-deployment finding — report 18 Jun 2024; period 1 Apr 2020–31 Mar 2023; SIIB agrees; Dec 2024 MAP milestone; current status **unknown** in vault.
