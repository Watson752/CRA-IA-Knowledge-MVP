# Test-03: Control Design and Operating Effectiveness (Post-Fix)

## Question

What is the difference between control design and operating effectiveness, and how would an auditor test each?

## Post-fix answer (vault-supported)

[[Design Effectiveness]] and [[Operating Effectiveness]] are first-class notes; [[Control]] links them with OE checklist dimensions (person/system, [[Control Frequency]], [[Audit Period]], evidence, consistency). [[Control Testing]] aggregates procedures: [[Walkthrough]], [[Inquiry]], [[Observation (Procedure)]], [[Inspection]], [[Reperformance]], [[Analytics]]. Explicit rules: inquiry alone ≠ OE; walkthrough ≠ period OE; documentation ≠ operation. [[Manual Control]] and [[Automated Control]] cover control types; automated reliance ties to [[IT Controls]] / [[System-Generated Evidence]].

Case anchors (illustrative, not full OE manuals): [[Internal Audit - Enterprise Fraud Management System]], [[Internal Audit - Charities Audit Process]], [[Internal Audit - Accounts Receivable National Inventory]].
