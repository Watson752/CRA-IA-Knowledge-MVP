# Test-01: Acronym Lookup (Branch Set)

## Question

What do AERB, CPB, ITB, SIIB, CVB, ABSB and LPRAB stand for, and what does each branch do?

## Answer

All seven acronyms resolve to **current** headquarters branch notes under `02-Organization/Branches/`, indexed in [[CRA-Acronym-Dictionary]], [[CRA-Program-Branches]], [[CRA-Corporate-Branches]], and [[CRA-Organization-Map]]. Mandates below are taken from those canonical notes and checked against the official Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page ([[99-Sources/source-notes/SRC-CRA-Org-2025]]). Named individuals on that page are treated as a **historical public snapshot**, not as confirmed current incumbents.

### Parallel-note / consistency issues observed

- Same Obsidian **title** collisions exist for Compliance Programs, Information Technology, and Collections and Verification (both `01-Organization/` and `02-Organization/Branches/`).
- Legacy AERB note filename/title omits Oxford commas (`Audit Evaluation and Risk Branch`) and lacks the `AERB` alias (uses `AER branch` instead).
- ABSB and LPRAB have no legacy `01-Organization/` parallel notes (canonical-only in `02-Organization/`).

## Recommended targeted fixes

Do **not** implement in this test. Suggested later work:

1. **Remove `SIIB` from aliases** on `01-Organization/Service Innovation and Integration Branch.md` (keep only the canonical pointer body).
2. **Disambiguate parallel titles** for CPB, ITB, and CVB (e.g., rename legacy notes to explicit “legacy / redirect” titles, or convert them to thin stubs without competing titles).
3. **Align legacy AERB naming** so searchers hitting `01-Organization/Audit Evaluation and Risk Branch.md` clearly land on [[Audit, Evaluation, and Risk Branch]] without an alternate acronym path (`AER branch`).
4. **Keep ABS caution visible** in intern paths: dictionary already marks ABS as verification required; ensure learning paths do not present ABS as a current branch acronym.
5. **Optional:** add one-line redirect-only stubs in `01-Organization/` for ABSB and LPRAB *or* explicitly document that those two are canonical-only under `02-Organization/` to avoid “missing branch” confusion during mixed-folder browsing.
