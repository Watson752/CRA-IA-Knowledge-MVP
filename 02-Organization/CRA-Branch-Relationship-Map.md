# CRA Branch Relationship Map

This map organizes **functions**, not unsupported reporting lines. For ownership vs assurance vocabulary see [[Ownership and Assurance Roles]]. Case matrix: [[Public-Audit-Case-Map]].

Every relationship is labelled:

- **Official public relationship** — stated or clearly demonstrated in official public sources
- **Derived onboarding relationship** — vault explanation aid
- **Historical relationship** — older public naming or period-specific context

## Tax and benefit processing

- [[Assessment, Benefit, and Service Branch|ABSB]] administers key tax processing services, contact centres, and benefits — **Official public relationship** (mandate).
- Regions operate Tax Centres and Contact Centres for delivery — **Official public relationship**.
- ABSB ↔ regions for processing/service channels — **Derived onboarding relationship**.

## Compliance

- [[Compliance Programs Branch|CPB]] delivers graduated compliance (education through investigations) — **Official public relationship**.
- CPB ↔ [[Business Intelligence]]/[[Information Technology Branch|ITB]]/[[Service, Innovation, and Integration Branch|SIIB]] for analytics-enabled compliance — **Derived onboarding relationship**, reinforced by BI audit stakeholder lists (**Official public relationship** for those named interviews).

## Collections and verification

- [[Collections and Verification Branch|CVB]] delivers validation, verification, and collections programs — **Official public relationship**.
- CVB ↔ regions for ARNI and inventory controls — **Official public relationship** ([[Internal Audit - Accounts Receivable National Inventory]]).
- CVB ↔ CPB as complementary integrity functions — **Derived onboarding relationship**.

## Appeals and recourse

- [[Appeals Branch]] delivers impartial recourse services — **Official public relationship**.
- Appeals ↔ compliance/collections decisions that generate disputes — **Derived onboarding relationship**.
- Appeals AC role includes tax-litigation risk management — **Official public relationship** (AC biography/mandate context on org page).

## Policy and legislative interpretation

- [[Legislative Policy and Regulatory Affairs Branch|LPRAB]] is ultimate authority for interpretation of tax/benefits legislation and recommends amendments to Finance — **Official public relationship**.
- Charities Directorate within LPRAB — **Official public relationship** ([[Internal Audit - Charities Audit Process]]).
- LPRAB ↔ Finance Canada on legislative change — **Official public relationship** (strategic relationships section).

## Service, data and integration

- [[Service, Innovation, and Integration Branch|SIIB]] leads service design, data/information assets, performance reporting, and partner coordination — **Official public relationship**.
- SIIB AC = Chief Data Officer and Chief Service Officer — **Official public relationship**.
- OLO in SIIB for Ombudsperson liaison — **Official public relationship**.
- SIIB BI responsibility since 2011 with ITB service delivery — **Official public relationship** (BI audit).

## Information technology

- [[Information Technology Branch|ITB]] develops/operates/evolves CRA IT and provides solutions to CBSA — **Official public relationship**.
- ITB ↔ SIIB for BI service delivery and BIDGSC co-chairing — **Official public relationship** (BI audit).
- ITB ↔ Security Branch for EFMS maintenance — **Official public relationship** (EFMS audit).

## Digital transformation

- [[Digital Transformation Program Branch|DTPB]] sets enterprise digital transformation direction — **Official public relationship**.
- DTPB ↔ ITB/SIIB on digital change — **Derived onboarding relationship** (DTPB also interviewed in BI audit: **Official public relationship** as stakeholder).

## Internal audit, evaluation and enterprise risk

- [[Audit, Evaluation, and Risk Branch|AERB]] provides independent assurance, evaluation, and enterprise risk support — **Official public relationship**.
- AERB publishes via [[Internal Audit and Program Evaluation]] — **Official public relationship**.
- AERB as third line in cyber audit narrative — **Official public relationship**.
- AERB ↔ Audit Committee / Board oversight context — **Official public relationship** / governance pages.

## Finance and administration

- [[02-Organization/Branches/Finance and Administration Branch]] provides financial/resource management, administration, real property; AC is CFO — **Official public relationship**.
- Financial statements reviewed by Audit Committee and approved by Board — **Official public relationship**.

## Human resources

- [[Human Resources Branch|HRB]] provides HR advice/services/stewardship — **Official public relationship**.
- HRB ↔ workforce capability for program delivery — **Derived onboarding relationship**.

## Communications and privacy

- [[Public Affairs Branch]] provides communications, ATIP, and related public affairs services; AC is Chief Privacy Officer — **Official public relationship**.
- Public Affairs ↔ confidentiality/privacy regime — **Derived onboarding relationship** (org-page confidentiality section + ATIP reports).

## Security

- [[02-Organization/Branches/Security Branch]] protects people, information, and assets; centralizes security functions — **Official public relationship**.
- Security Branch formation / ASO role increase as of April 2022 — **Official public relationship** (cyber audit).
- Security ↔ ITB ↔ AERB Three Lines description — **Official public relationship** (cyber audit).

## Legal services

- [[Legal Services Branch]] / Department of Justice provides litigation and legal advisory services to CRA, Board, and Ombudsperson — **Official public relationship**.
- Legal Services ↔ Appeals litigation-risk themes — **Derived onboarding relationship**.

## Regional delivery

- [[Atlantic Region]], [[Quebec Region]], [[Ontario Region]], [[Western Region]] deliver programs via field offices — **Official public relationship**.
- Regions ↔ HQ program branches (technical/policy support) — **Official public relationship**.
- Regions ↔ corporate branches (enterprise services) — **Derived onboarding relationship**.

## Historical relationships

- [[Domestic Compliance Programs Branch]] — **Historical relationship**; do not equate to [[Compliance Programs Branch|CPB]] without an official succession source.
- Older transition pages listing **13** HQ branches — **Historical relationship**; current public baseline is **14** branches (2025).
