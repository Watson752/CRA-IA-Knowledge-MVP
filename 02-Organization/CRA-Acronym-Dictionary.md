## Acronym verification rule

Acronyms are added as aliases only when an official CRA public source (organization page, published audit/evaluation, departmental results material, or similarly authoritative canada.ca page) uses or clearly establishes them. Do not invent acronyms from name initials alone.

Only acronyms supported by official public CRA/Government of Canada sources or existing vault case notes grounded in those sources. Status markers:

- **Status: current** — used in current official public materials
- **Status: historical** — appears in older public materials; not treated as current structure
- **Status: verification required** — seen in vault/report language but needs cautious use

## Naming cautions

- Do not silently treat historical branch names as current.
- Prefer full branch titles in prose; use acronyms after first introduction.
- Prefer role notes over person-centric notes for the knowledge graph.
