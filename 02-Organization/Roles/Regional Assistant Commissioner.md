# Regional Assistant Commissioner

## Role (not a person)

A **Regional Assistant Commissioner (RAC)** leads one of the four CRA regions responsible for program delivery.

## Public responsibilities

- Lead regional operations for Atlantic, Quebec, Ontario, or Western regions ([[CRA-Regions]]).
- Regions deliver programs via field offices (TSOs, TCs, NVCCs, contact centres, and related public office types).

## Legend (official)

Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page legend: **RAC: Regional Assistant Commissioner**. The page also uses **a/RAC** for acting appointments.

## Current office holders

Named RACs on the 2025 transition page should be re-verified; acting appointments are time-limited.


## Office holders

### Current office holder

Role title is stable. Individual incumbents are branch- or region-specific and must be confirmed on a current official page. Names on the 2025 organizational chart are **historical snapshot** only — see branch and region notes.

### Historical office holders

See the 2025 Organization page chart and the relevant [[CRA-Organizational-Overview]] conflict table. Do not promote chart names to “current” without a newer official confirmation.
