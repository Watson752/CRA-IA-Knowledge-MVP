# Corporate Secretariat

## Classification

**Other organizational component** — not one of the 14 headquarters branches. Official source states the CRA comprises 14 headquarters branches, a **Corporate Secretariat**, and 4 regions.

## Public mandate

Reporting to the Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner’s offices, the Corporate Secretariat assists the Board of Management, the Commissioner, the Deputy Commissioner, and senior management in decision-making by providing governance, logistic, and planning services to Chairs, members, and committees. It also supports management and oversight of CRA corporate policy instruments (CPIs) and is the Agency’s centre of expertise for Governor in Council appointments.

## Board Secretariat relationship

Officially supported: Board operations are supported by the Board Secretariat, led by a Director who reports to the Corporate Secretary, who reports directly to the Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner.

## Why a new employee should understand it

Corporate Secretariat explains how Board/committee logistics and corporate policy instruments are supported without confusing the Secretariat with AERB (assurance) or Public Affairs (communications/ATIP).
