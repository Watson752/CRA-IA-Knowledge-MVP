# Deputy Assistant Commissioner

## Role (not a person)

A **Deputy Assistant Commissioner (DAC)** is a senior executive publicly listed for some headquarters branches in support of the Assistant Commissioner.

## Public responsibilities

- Exercise duties publicly associated with deputy branch leadership where named (examples on the org page include Agency Comptroller in Finance and Administration; deputy leadership in ABSB, HRB, and ITB).

## Legend (official)

Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page legend: **DAC: Deputy Assistant Commissioner**.

## Current office holders

Named DACs on transition pages can change. Verify against the latest official CRA organization materials before relying on a name.


## Office holders

### Current office holder

Role title is stable. Individual incumbents are branch- or region-specific and must be confirmed on a current official page. Names on the 2025 organizational chart are **historical snapshot** only — see branch and region notes.

### Historical office holders

See the 2025 Organization page chart and the relevant [[CRA-Organizational-Overview]] conflict table. Do not promote chart names to “current” without a newer official confirmation.
