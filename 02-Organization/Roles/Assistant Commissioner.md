# Assistant Commissioner

## Role (not a person)

An **Assistant Commissioner (AC)** is a senior CRA executive who leads a headquarters program or corporate branch (or holds an equivalent senior title publicly listed for a branch).

## Public responsibilities

- Lead a headquarters branch listed on the CRA organization overview.
- Some ACs hold additional public titles (examples from the official org page): Chief Audit Executive; Chief Financial Officer; Chief Information Officer; Chief Data Officer and Chief Service Officer; Chief Privacy Officer; Digital Transformation Officer; Chief Human Resources Officer; Agency Security Officer.

## Legend (official)

Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page legend: **AC: Assistant Commissioner**.

## Current office holders

Office holders change. See individual branch notes and the official organization page for named ACs. Do not treat any single AC name as permanent without checking a current official source.

## Why a new employee should understand it

Public audits and departmental plans often name an AC-led branch as program owner, MAP respondent, or stakeholder. Knowing the AC role helps you separate **branch accountability** from **individual incumbents**.


## Office holders

### Current office holder

Role title is stable. Individual incumbents are branch- or region-specific and must be confirmed on a current official page. Names on the 2025 organizational chart are **historical snapshot** only — see branch and region notes.

### Historical office holders

See the 2025 Organization page chart and the relevant [[CRA-Organizational-Overview]] conflict table. Do not promote chart names to “current” without a newer official confirmation.
