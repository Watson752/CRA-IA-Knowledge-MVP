## Stable public structure

The CRA comprises:

- **14 headquarters branches**
- a [[Corporate Secretariat]]
- **4 regions**

Split:

- **6 program branches** — technical and policy support for program delivery → [[CRA-Program-Branches]]
- **8 corporate branches** — corporate services → [[CRA-Corporate-Branches]]
- **4 regions** — program delivery via field offices → [[CRA-Regions]]
