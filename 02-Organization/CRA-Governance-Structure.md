# CRA Governance Structure

## Stable role information (official)

Primary baseline: Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page (chart, chart description, and Agency Governance Overview).

The CRA governance structure includes:

- a [[Minister of National Revenue]]
- a [[Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer]] (Deputy Minister equivalent)
- a [[Deputy Commissioner]] (Associate Deputy Minister equivalent)
- a [[Board of Management]]
- a [[Taxpayers Ombudsperson]] (arm’s length; accountable directly to the Minister; no authority in administration/enforcement of legislation for which CRA remains accountable to the Minister)

Supporting governance component:

- [[Corporate Secretariat]] — assists Board, Commissioner, Deputy Commissioner, and senior management; supports corporate policy instruments; centre of expertise for GIC appointments

Official legend titles:

- [[Assistant Commissioner|AC]]
- [[Deputy Assistant Commissioner|DAC]]
- [[Regional Assistant Commissioner|RAC]]
- **ADM** — Assistant Deputy Minister
- **ED/SGC** — Executive Director/Senior General Counsel

## Board of Management (stable)

- Oversees organization and administration of the CRA and management of resources, services, property, personnel, and contracts
- Develops/approves Corporate Business Plan; approves Departmental Results Report and annual audited financial statements
- May advise the Minister on general administration/enforcement of program legislation
- May **not** direct the Commissioner or an employee in statutory powers or administration/enforcement of program legislation
- Not authorized to receive personal/business information obtained under program legislation

See [[Board of Management]] · [[Audit Committee]].

## Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner (stable)

**Commissioner and CEO**

- Day-to-day management and direction of the CRA
- Accountable to the Minister, the Board, and the Clerk of the Privy Council
- Ex-officio Board member

**Deputy Commissioner**

- Supports the Commissioner; exercises assigned duties; acts for the Commissioner in absence, incapacity, or vacancy
- Not a Board member but normally attends Board meetings

## Taxpayers’ Ombudsperson (stable)

- Arm’s length from CRA operations
- Accountable directly to the Minister
- Service-complaint, systemic reporting, and outreach mandate
- Official organizational relationship: Ombudsperson Liaison Office located in [[Service, Innovation, and Integration Branch|SIIB]]; SIIB AC (as Chief Service Officer) holds monthly bilateral meetings with the Ombudsperson
