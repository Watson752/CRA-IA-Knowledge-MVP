# Quebec Region

## Geographic scope

The province of Quebec. Official source notes it is the only region where taxpayers must deal with two tax authorities.

## Public program-delivery role

One of four CRA regions responsible for **program delivery via field offices** in their area of operation. Each region is led by a [[Regional Assistant Commissioner]].

Official public source describes regional operations in:

- **Tax Services Offices (TSOs)** — more complex audit, collection and related files with more direct taxpayer/representative interaction
- **Tax Centres (TCs)** — tax return processing centres
- **National Verification and Collections Centres (NVCCs)** — non-complex, non-face-to-face collections, validation, and verification
- **Contact Centres (CC)** — individual or business enquiries for tax and benefits
- **Northern Service Centres (NSC)** — three NSCs support individuals, community organizations and businesses in Yukon, Northwest Territories and Nunavut
- **Centres of Expertise (COEs)** — leadership, best practices, research, support and training for a focus area

Do not infer staffing structures beyond this public listing.

## Relationship with headquarters branches

### Officially supported relationships

- Regions deliver operational programs while six headquarters **program** branches centrally organize technical and policy support ([[CRA-Program-Branches]]).
- Eight headquarters **corporate** branches provide corporate services agency-wide ([[CRA-Corporate-Branches]]).

### Derived onboarding interpretation

> Not an official reporting-line diagram.

- Headquarters program branches set national program frameworks; regions execute workload through TSOs, TCs, NVCCs, and contact centres.
- Corporate branches (for example [[Information Technology Branch|ITB]] and [[Security Branch]]) support enterprise capabilities used in regional delivery.
