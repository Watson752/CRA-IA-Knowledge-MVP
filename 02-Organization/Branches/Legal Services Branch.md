# Legal Services Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Legal Services Branch
- **Acronym:** Not established as a standard short form in the official public sources used for this vault. Do not invent one.

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **corporate branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

The Department of Justice Canada is solely responsible for providing litigation and legal advisory services to all branches of the CRA, the CRA’s Board of Management, and the Taxpayers’ Ombudsperson. Justice Counsel of the CRA Legal Services Branch provide legal advisory services to the CRA.

## Main public responsibilities

- Legal advisory services to CRA via Justice counsel in the CRA Legal Services Branch.
- Litigation and legal advisory services provided by Department of Justice Canada to CRA, Board, and Ombudsperson.

## Why a new employee should understand it

Legal Services clarifies that CRA legal advice/litigation is a Justice Canada responsibility delivered through the CRA Legal Services Branch—important when reading appeals, litigation-risk, or enforcement narratives.
