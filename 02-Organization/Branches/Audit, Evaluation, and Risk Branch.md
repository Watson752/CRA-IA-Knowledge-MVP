# Audit, Evaluation, and Risk Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Audit, Evaluation, and Risk Branch
- **Acronym:** AERB (Status: current — supported by official public CRA sources and/or published CRA audit/evaluation reports)
- Preferred short link: [[Audit, Evaluation, and Risk Branch|AERB]]

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **corporate branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

Enables achievement of CRA strategic goals by providing independent and objective information, advice and assurance on the soundness of the CRA management framework and on the effectiveness, efficiency and economy of its strategies, programs and practices. Ensures a holistic approach to managing risk and fosters responsible risk taking and continuous improvement.

## Main public responsibilities

- Independent internal audit and program evaluation functions.
- Enterprise risk management oversight and advice.
- Chief Audit Executive leadership; Senior Officer for internal disclosure of wrongdoing (publicly stated for the AC/CAE role).
- Publishes audit and evaluation results for public accountability ([[Internal Audit and Program Evaluation]]).

### Independence clarification (onboarding)

> **Derived onboarding interpretation** (not a change to the official mandate text above): AERB’s enterprise risk **oversight and advice** role is not the same as first-line ownership of program risks and controls, and it is not MAP execution. Management owns risks, controls, and corrective action; AERB provides independent assurance and may follow up ([[Internal Audit Independence]], [[Ownership and Assurance Roles]], [[Three Lines Model]]). Judging that management action plans are “reasonable” is an assurance judgment—not proof that remediation is complete or that AERB operates the control.

## Why a new employee should understand it

AERB is the public third-line assurance home for published CRA internal audits and evaluations. Almost every case in the vault is an AERB product; knowing AERB clarifies independence, MAP follow-up, and Board/Audit Committee governance.

## Related branches

### Officially supported relationships

- Corporate headquarters branch ([[CRA-Corporate-Branches]]).
- Assistant Commissioner is Chief Audit Executive.
- Board/Audit Committee oversight context for IA/PE planning and reporting.
- Official public sources use AERB (e.g., departmental results financial discussion; historical IA of ERM describing 2013 merger creating AERB).

### Derived onboarding interpretation

> These connections are vault onboarding aids. They are **not** official CRA reporting lines.

- Assurance over program and corporate branches without replacing management ownership of controls (Three Lines interpretation).
