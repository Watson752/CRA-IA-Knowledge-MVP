# Appeals Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Appeals Branch
- **Acronym:** Not established as a standard short form in the official public sources used for this vault. Do not invent one.

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **program branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

To deliver high quality, timely, and impartial recourse services.

## Main public responsibilities

- Delivers CRA recourse programs for taxpayers seeking review of CRA decisions.
- Leads risk management related to tax litigation (publicly stated for the Assistant Commissioner role).

## Why a new employee should understand it

Knowing Appeals helps you place formal review and recourse pathways beside compliance and collections work, and interpret audit or evaluation findings that mention litigation risk or taxpayer rights to review.
