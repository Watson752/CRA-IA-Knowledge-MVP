# Digital Transformation Program Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Digital Transformation Program Branch
- **Acronym:** DTPB (Status: current — supported by official public CRA sources and/or published CRA audit/evaluation reports)
- Preferred short link: [[Digital Transformation Program Branch|DTPB]]

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **corporate branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

> **Beginner caution:** Despite “Program” in the branch name, DTPB is a **corporate** branch (enterprise digital transformation direction), not one of the six headquarters **program** branches. See [[CRA-Corporate-Branches]] vs [[CRA-Program-Branches]].

## Public mandate

Builds a unified direction for enterprise-wide digital transformation and creates an environment without barriers that keeps pace with change.

## Main public responsibilities

- Enterprise digital transformation direction beyond technology alone (processes and skills).
- Led by Assistant Commissioner and Digital Transformation Officer (public org page).

## Why a new employee should understand it

DTPB explains why digital change appears as a program of work distinct from day-to-day IT operations (ITB). Useful when reading BI, service, and transformation themes in plans and audits.
