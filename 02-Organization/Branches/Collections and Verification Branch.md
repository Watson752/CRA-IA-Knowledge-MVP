# Collections and Verification Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Collections and Verification Branch
- **Acronym:** CVB (Status: current — supported by official public CRA sources and/or published CRA audit/evaluation reports)
- Preferred short link: [[Collections and Verification Branch|CVB]]

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **program branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

Protects the integrity and fairness of Canada's tax system for all Canadians by delivering national validation, compliance, and collections programs that foster understanding and voluntary compliance; validate returns and benefit entitlements; identify and address non-compliance using verification techniques and analytics; and collect outstanding debts through progressive and targeted enforcement.

## Main public responsibilities

- National validation, compliance, and collections programs.
- Validation of tax returns and benefit entitlements.
- Analytics-enabled verification and progressive debt collection.

## Why a new employee should understand it

CVB owns publicly described collections and verification programs such as ARNI. Understanding CVB is essential when reading collections audits, inventory metrics, decision-support systems, and regional workload interactions.
