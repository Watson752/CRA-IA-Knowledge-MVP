# Public Affairs Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Public Affairs Branch
- **Acronym:** Not established as a standard short form in the official public sources used for this vault. Do not invent one.

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **corporate branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

Provides effective public affairs advice, products, and services to the CRA and its clients in communications, issues management, brand management, ministerial services, parliamentary and Cabinet affairs, access to information and privacy, public opinion research, internet planning, and publishing.

## Main public responsibilities

- Communications, issues and brand management.
- Ministerial, parliamentary, and Cabinet affairs support.
- Access to Information and Privacy (ATIP).
- Public opinion research, internet planning, and publishing.
- Chief Privacy Officer leadership (AC and CPO).

## Why a new employee should understand it

Public Affairs (including ATIP and the Chief Privacy Officer) helps you read privacy, transparency, and external-communications responsibilities that appear in ATIP annual reports and confidentiality frameworks.
