# Finance and Administration Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Finance and Administration Branch
- **Acronym:** Not established as a standard short form in the official public sources used for this vault. Do not invent one.

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **corporate branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

Facilitates the delivery of CRA programs and services by providing sound advice, products and services in diversified key functions: namely, financial and resource management, administration and real property.

## Main public responsibilities

- Financial and resource management.
- Administration and real property services.
- Chief Financial Officer leadership (AC and CFO).
- Agency Comptroller role (DAC).

## Why a new employee should understand it

Finance and Administration is the public home of CFO/comptrollership functions that appear in financial statements, resource allocation, and Board approval of audited financial statements.

### Officially supported relationships
