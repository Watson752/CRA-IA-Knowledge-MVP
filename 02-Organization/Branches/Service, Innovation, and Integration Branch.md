# Service, Innovation, and Integration Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Service, Innovation, and Integration Branch
- **Acronym:** SIIB (Status: current — supported by official public CRA sources and/or published CRA audit/evaluation reports)
- Preferred short link: [[Service, Innovation, and Integration Branch|SIIB]]

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **program branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

Leads cross-cutting CRA projects for an integrated enterprise approach; improves service design and delivery with client centricity and accessibility; manages CRA data and information assets and related governance/quality frameworks; conducts strategic planning, performance measurement, and reporting; and coordinates relationships with federal, provincial, territorial, and Indigenous partners.

## Main public responsibilities

- Enterprise integration and cross-cutting projects.
- Service design/delivery improvement and client feedback journeys.
- Data/information asset management, governance, and quality standards.
- Agency strategic planning, performance measurement, and reporting.
- Partner relationship coordination; hosts Ombudsperson Liaison Office (OLO).

## Why a new employee should understand it

SIIB is where service, data, and enterprise performance reporting publicly meet. The Chief Data Officer and Chief Service Officer roles sit with the SIIB Assistant Commissioner. BI governance audits and many data-strategy notes point here.

## Related branches

### Officially supported relationships

- Program headquarters branch ([[CRA-Program-Branches]]).
- Assistant Commissioner is also Chief Data Officer and Chief Service Officer (official org page).
- Ombudsperson Liaison Office located in SIIB; monthly bilateral meetings with the Ombudsperson as Chief Service Officer.
