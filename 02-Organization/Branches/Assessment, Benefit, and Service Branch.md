# Assessment, Benefit, and Service Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Assessment, Benefit, and Service Branch
- **Acronym:** ABSB (Status: current — supported by official public CRA sources and/or published CRA audit/evaluation reports)
- Preferred short link: [[Assessment, Benefit, and Service Branch|ABSB]]

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **program branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

Administer key tax processing services and contact centre operations, enable tax and revenue generation programs, and administer benefits on behalf of the federal government and most provinces.

## Main public responsibilities

- Tax processing services and contact centre operations.
- Enabling tax and revenue generation programs.
- Benefits administration for the federal government and most provinces.

## Why a new employee should understand it

ABSB is the public home of assessment, benefits, and large-scale service channels. New employees need it to interpret departmental results on benefits and service, contact-centre themes, and cross-branch audits that involve processing or benefit entitlement validation.
