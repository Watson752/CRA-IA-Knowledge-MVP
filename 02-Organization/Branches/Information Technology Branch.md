# Information Technology Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Information Technology Branch
- **Acronym:** ITB (Status: current — supported by official public CRA sources and/or published CRA audit/evaluation reports)
- Preferred short link: [[Information Technology Branch|ITB]]

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **corporate branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

Develops, operates, maintains and evolves the CRA’s information technology (IT), with the goal of delivering world-class technology services and solutions that meet the expectations of employees and Canadians. Also provides client computing and corporate IT solutions to the Canada Border Services Agency.

## Main public responsibilities

- Develop, operate, maintain, and evolve CRA IT services and solutions.
- Provide client computing and corporate IT solutions to CBSA.
- Chief Information Officer leadership.

## Why a new employee should understand it

ITB is the public enterprise IT owner/operator. Cyber, EFMS, BI service delivery, and digital-service themes in audits almost always require ITB literacy even when MAP owners sit elsewhere.

**Ownership caution:** Technical support and system maintenance roles are **not** automatic business-process ownership. See [[Technical Support]], [[System Owner]], and [[Ownership and Assurance Roles]].
