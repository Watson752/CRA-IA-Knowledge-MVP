# Human Resources Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Human Resources Branch
- **Acronym:** HRB (Status: current — supported by official public CRA sources and/or published CRA audit/evaluation reports)
- Preferred short link: [[Human Resources Branch|HRB]]

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **corporate branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

Provides HR advice, services, and stewardship that support the Agency in achieving its business priorities.

## Main public responsibilities

- HR advice, services, and stewardship.
- Chief Human Resources Officer leadership.

## Why a new employee should understand it

HRB helps place workforce capability, equity/diversity initiatives, and people-related priorities that appear in departmental plans and Board human-resources committee language.
