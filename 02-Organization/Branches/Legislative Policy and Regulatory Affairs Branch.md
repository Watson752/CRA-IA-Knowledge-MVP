# Legislative Policy and Regulatory Affairs Branch

## Full name and acronym

- **Full name:** Legislative Policy and Regulatory Affairs Branch
- **Acronym:** LPRAB (Status: current — supported by official public CRA sources and/or published CRA audit/evaluation reports)
- Preferred short link: [[Legislative Policy and Regulatory Affairs Branch|LPRAB]]

## Classification

Publicly classified as a **program branch** among CRA headquarters branches (Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page: 6 program / 8 corporate headquarters branches, plus Corporate Secretariat and 4 regions).

## Public mandate

Acts as the ultimate authority for the interpretation of tax and benefits related legislation. Establishes and clarifies the interpretation of tax laws and is responsible for recommending legislative amendments to the Department of Finance on behalf of the CRA. Holds responsibility for registering and regulating charities and the income tax treatment of registered plans, and for excise and specialty tax programs.

## Main public responsibilities

- Authoritative interpretation of tax and benefits legislation; GST/HST rulings and legislative policy.
- Charities registration/regulation and registered plans.
- Excise and specialty tax program administration (excise duty/tax, fuel charge, luxury tax, air travelers security charge).
- Recommending legislative amendments to the Department of Finance on behalf of the CRA.

## Why a new employee should understand it

LPRAB explains why charities, rulings, and legislative interpretation appear in audits separately from Compliance Programs. The Charities Audit Process case is an LPRAB MAP owner example.
