# CRA Regions

Primary baseline: Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page (regions section + chart). Named RACs on the 2025 chart are a **historical public snapshot**.

Official public sources identify **four regions** responsible for program delivery via field offices:

1. [[Atlantic Region]]
2. [[Quebec Region]]
3. [[Ontario Region]]
4. [[Western Region]]

Each region is led by a [[Regional Assistant Commissioner]].

## Public office types used in regional delivery

Described on the Ministerial Transition 2025 Organization page:

- Tax Services Offices (TSOs)
- Tax Centres (TCs)
- National Verification and Collections Centres (NVCCs)
- Contact Centres (CC)
- Northern Service Centres (NSC)
- Centres of Expertise (COEs)

## Headquarters relationship

- Headquarters **program** branches provide technical and policy support ([[CRA-Program-Branches]]).
- Headquarters **corporate** branches provide corporate services ([[CRA-Corporate-Branches]]).
- Regions execute delivery in geographic areas.

**Onboarding pair:** HQ program branches **support**; regions **deliver** via field offices. This is a functional distinction, not an invented reporting-line diagram.
