# Grounded Audit Inquiry Guidelines

> **Derived vault governance** for future AI or Cursor answers over this corpus. Complements [[Public-Source-RAG-Grounding]] and [[Content-Classification-Model]].

## Mandatory behaviour

1. **Retrieve before answering** — search maps and source-linked notes; prefer `00-Start/*Map*`, ownership primers, case notes, and bridges.
2. **Use source-linked notes** — cite vault notes and, for official facts, original public URLs on those notes.
3. **Distinguish content classes** — official public facts; general professional knowledge; derived interpretation; synthetic examples.
4. **Historical cases = precedents, not proof** — follow [[Interpreting Historical Public Audit Findings]] / [[How Historical Findings Should Be Used as Precedent]].
5. **Propose lines of inquiry, not findings** — risk indicators ≠ findings. A [[Finding]] requires condition vs [[Criteria]] with sufficient appropriate [[Evidence]].
6. **Identify missing evidence** — state what internal evidence would be required.
7. **State uncertainty** — especially when follow-up status is unknown.
8. **Do not use synthetic content as evidence** — Class D / synthetic demos and teaching scenarios are not CRA facts.
9. **Preserve access and classification boundaries** — never invent protected, taxpayer, or non-public detail; respect redactions.
10. **Cite supporting notes and original sources** — include wikilinks; for official claims, point to case/org source URLs.

## Forbidden

- Declaring non-compliance, fraud, or control failure from symptoms alone  
- Inferring current CRA conditions from historical reports  
- Assigning unsupported ownership of non-public systems  
- Portraying AERB as operating program controls or owning MAP execution  
- Implying ITB owns program outcomes merely because technology is involved  

## Preferred answer shape

```text
Risk indicator → line of inquiry → evidence request → audit procedure
→ analysis → possible observation (not finding)
```

Full engagement lifecycle when scoping:

```text
Objective → Scope → Criteria → Risk → Control → Procedure
→ Evidence → Finding → Recommendation → Management Action → Follow-Up
```
