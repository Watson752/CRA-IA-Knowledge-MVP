**Siloed knowledge** exists when critical know-how, data definitions, or analytical results remain isolated in teams, personal drives, or legacy systems— unknown or unusable elsewhere. Silos increase [[Risk]] from inconsistent decisions, [[Outdated Analytics]], and loss of expertise ([[Knowledge Continuity]] risks).

Technology alone does not fix silos; [[Governance]], incentives, and [[Horizontal Collaboration]] matter. Documented metadata, shared repositories, and certified BI products counter silo effects for [[Structured Data]]; [[Unstructured Information]] needs records management.

Auditors encounter silos when interviewees contradict each other’s metrics or when the same question requires multiple incompatible extracts. Findings may address documentation, stewardship, or enterprise repositories— framed generally unless case evidence supports specifics.
