**Rare high-impact events** occur infrequently but carry severe compliance, security, financial, or fairness consequences. Counts may be too small for stable rates or significance tests ([[Small-Cell Analysis]]), yet each event can be operationally decisive ([[Operational Significance]], [[Materiality]]).

Do **not** treat lack of [[Statistical Significance]] as proof of acceptable risk. Prefer certainty testing, [[Risk-Based Selection]], and qualitative evaluation against [[Criteria]]. [[False Negatives]] remind that low alert volume is not proof of good control.
