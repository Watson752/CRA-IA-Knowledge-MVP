**Data governance** is the exercise of authority and control over data assets— policies, standards, ownership, quality management, lifecycle, privacy, and security. It clarifies [[Roles and Responsibilities]] for data stewards, custodians, and consumers. Effective governance improves [[Data Quality]], lawful use, and trust in [[Analytics]] and [[Business Intelligence]].

Governance bodies prioritize critical data elements, resolve definition disputes, and oversee metadata and lineage. It connects to [[Information and Data Strategy]] and enterprise [[Governance]]. Public sector contexts add transparency, official languages, and statistical publication rules where applicable.

Auditors assess whether governance is operational (decisions documented, issues remediated) or nominal (policies only). Data used as [[Evidence]] should trace to governed sources with known [[Assessment Cut-Off Date]] and quality attributes.

Data governance supports [[Risk Algorithms]] and [[Artificial Intelligence]] by defining training data standards and monitoring responsibilities.
