**Missing values** are blank, null, or unusable fields on records that otherwise exist. Distinct from [[Missing Records]]. Still a form of [[Missing Data]] that can weaken [[Data Quality]] and [[Evidence Reliability]] when critical attributes for the [[Criteria]] are absent.

Not every missing value creates population incompleteness; assess whether the gap affects precision, validity, or both ([[Missing Data]]).
