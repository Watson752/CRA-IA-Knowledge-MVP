**Selection bias** arises when the process that creates, filters, or selects records systematically favours some units, so the analysed group is unrepresentative of the [[Intended Population]]. Examples: voluntary response, filtered extracts, convenience picks, risk-based sets treated as random.

Distinct from **sampling variability** under a valid design ([[Sampling Risk]]): chance error vs systematic distortion from how the frame/sample was built ([[Systematic Exclusion]], [[Data Pipeline]] filters).

Not every instance of [[Missing Data]] creates selection bias—random missingness mainly reduces precision; systematic missingness threatens validity ([[Missing Data]]).
