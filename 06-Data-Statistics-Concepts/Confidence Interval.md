A **confidence interval** is a range of plausible values for a population parameter (or projected rate) consistent with the sample/design and stated confidence level. Often more informative for audit communication than a binary significant/non-significant label ([[Statistical Significance]]).

Requires a statistical design and sound [[Sampling Frame]] for [[Statistical Extrapolation]]. Judgmental samples normally do not justify population confidence intervals. Keep case-specific levels (e.g. Audit Yield segment **95%**) bound to their reports—not as a vault-wide rule.

Uncertainty and directional language also appear in [[How Statistical Limitations Affect Audit Conclusions]] and [[Reasonable Assurance]].
