**Rounding** adjusts numeric values to fixed precision for presentation, storage efficiency, or disclosure control. Published statistics may round counts or dollars to nearest thousand, creating small differences between detail and totals. Rounding is not necessarily error but must be documented so users interpret figures correctly.

Auditors reconciling reports to subledger detail should know rounding conventions to avoid false [[Finding]]s. Combined with [[Data Suppression]], rounded tables may not foot mechanically across dimensions.

In analytics, inconsistent rounding between systems can create reconciliation noise; governance may specify standard precision for [[Performance Reporting]].

Rounding policies should appear in statistical or report footnotes; see [[Comparability Across Editions]] when methods change.
