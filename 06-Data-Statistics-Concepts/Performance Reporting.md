**Performance reporting** communicates results against plans, indicators, and [[Enterprise Objectives]]— internally via dashboards and externally via departmental results reports, statistical publications, or regulatory filings. Reports should define indicators, baselines, targets, and measurement methods clearly.

Public sector performance reporting often links planned results to actual outcomes and explains variances. Quality depends on indicator governance, data timeliness, and consistent methodology across reporting periods ([[Comparability Across Editions]]).

Auditors compare performance claims to underlying [[Evidence]], test calculations, and assess whether known limitations ([[Data Suppression]], [[Rounding]], revisions) are disclosed. Misalignment between internal [[Business Intelligence]] and external reports is a common review theme.

Performance reporting supports accountability and [[Evidence-Based Decision-Making]] when metrics are trustworthy.
