**Missing records** are entire in-scope items that never appear in the extract or register. Distinct from [[Missing Values]] (blank fields on present rows). A primary form of [[Missing Data]] that threatens [[Population Completeness]] and can bias rates if omissions are systematic ([[Selection Bias]], [[Systematic Exclusion]]).
