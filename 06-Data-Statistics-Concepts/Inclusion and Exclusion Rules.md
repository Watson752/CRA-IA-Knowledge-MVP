**Inclusion and exclusion rules** define which items belong in the [[Intended Population]] and which are out of scope (categories, statuses, dollar thresholds, channels, periods). Document them in methodology so the [[Sampling Frame]] and analytics filters can be checked against the engagement design ([[Scope]], [[Assessment Cut-Off Date]]).

Silent filters in a [[Data Pipeline]] that do not match documented rules create [[Systematic Exclusion]] and threaten [[Population Completeness]].
