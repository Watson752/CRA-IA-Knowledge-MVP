**Survivorship bias** is the distortion from examining only completed, active, successful, or retained cases while excluding failures, withdrawals, rejects, dormant items, or never-loaded records. Outcomes and controls can look stronger than they are in the full cohort.

Adjacent vault themes: include [[Dormant Accounts]] in access extracts; inspect [[Rejected Records]]; do not treat alert-only views as complete detection ([[False Negatives]]). Analysing only open/closed operational files may miss treatment coverage needed for a conclusion—without relabeling public reports as “survivorship” findings unless they use that term.
