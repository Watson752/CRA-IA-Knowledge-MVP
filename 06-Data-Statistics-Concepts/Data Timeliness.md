**Data timeliness** is whether data are available when needed and reflect the intended time window—a [[Data Quality]] dimension distinct from accuracy and completeness. Interacts with [[Assessment Cut-Off Date]] and pipeline latency ([[Batch Processing]], [[Performance Reporting]]).
