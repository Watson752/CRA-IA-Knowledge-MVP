**Override population analytics** profiles override/exception events after [[Population Completeness]] of the override frame is tested:

1. Frequency and trends by period/rule/type  
2. Concentration by user, unit, region, case type  
3. Reason-code quality (blank/generic)  
4. Outcomes / dispositions after override  
5. Feedback to rule quality ([[False Positives]] / [[False Negatives]])

High legitimate override volume may signal false-positive pressure—not automatic abuse. Low volume ≠ good control if false negatives are silent. Use [[Full-Population Analysis]] when structured data allow; [[Stratified Sampling]] for deep approval/outcome tests.
