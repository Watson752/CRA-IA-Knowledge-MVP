**Business intelligence** (BI) is the discipline of turning organizational data into actionable insight for planning and operations. It combines data integration, modeling, reporting, and visualization so leaders can monitor [[Enterprise Objectives]] and performance. BI emphasizes recurring metrics and dashboards, often built on curated datasets rather than one-off analyses.

BI success depends on [[Data Governance]], consistent definitions, and [[Business Intelligence Governance]] that certifies official metrics. Without governance, parallel spreadsheets and [[Duplicate Analytical Work]] proliferate.

BI complements [[Analytics]] and [[Research]]: BI tends toward standardized KPIs; analytics may explore hypotheses; research may investigate causality or program theory. [[Business Intelligence Tools]] implement BI capabilities technically.

Auditors use BI outputs cautiously as [[Evidence]]— verifying lineage, [[Data Quality]], and alignment with [[Performance Reporting]] to external stakeholders.
