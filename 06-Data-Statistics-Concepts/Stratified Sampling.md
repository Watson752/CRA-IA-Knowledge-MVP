**Stratified sampling** partitions the [[Sampling Frame]] into strata (e.g. value bands, risk tiers, rule versions, periods) and then samples **within each stratum**—often randomly—to improve [[Representativeness]] or allocate effort to important segments. Stratification is **not** the same as [[Judgmental Sampling]] / picking only high-risk items without within-stratum selection.

High-value or high-risk items may form a certainty stratum (100% tested) while other strata are randomly sampled—see [[Evaluation - Audit Yield]] (official: all files ≥ $5 million plus stratified random sample of others; **95%** confidence for the **sampled segment** only).

Interpret within-stratum results for that stratum; combine across strata only when the design supports it. Sparse strata raise [[Sampling Risk]] ([[Small-Cell Analysis]]). Still depends on [[Population Completeness]] of the frame.
