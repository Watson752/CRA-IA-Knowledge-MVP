**Sensitivity analysis** tests whether conclusions change when incomplete segments are excluded, bounded, or alternately assumed. A mitigation when [[Missing Data]], [[Selection Bias]], or uncertain parameters exist ([[How Statistical Limitations Affect Audit Conclusions]], [[Missing Data]] procedures).

Use before strong population-rate claims; document scenarios and resulting wording changes for the [[Audit Conclusion]].
