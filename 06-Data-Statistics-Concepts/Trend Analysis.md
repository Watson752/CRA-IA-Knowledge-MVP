**Trend analysis** examines how metrics or rates change over time. Patterns are hypotheses—not findings—until corroborated ([[Evidence]], [[Analytics]] association ≠ causation).

Before interpreting a break or slope, check context:

- changing definitions ([[Comparability Across Editions]]);
- denominators / population composition;
- [[Assessment Cut-Off Date]] and late data ([[Statistical Revision]]);
- seasonality / calendar operations;
- policy or [[Change Management|system/rule changes]];
- [[Data Quality]] / completeness shifts ([[Missing Data]]).

Misleading trends often reflect process or definition change rather than control failure ([[How Statistical Limitations Affect Audit Conclusions]]). Prioritise investigation with [[Materiality]] and [[Operational Significance]].
