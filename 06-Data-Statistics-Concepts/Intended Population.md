The **intended population** is every item that should be in scope for the engagement given the [[Audit Objective]], [[Scope]], [[Audit Period]], [[Criteria]], and documented [[Inclusion and Exclusion Rules]].

It is the universe conclusions claim to cover—unless wording is narrowed to a [[Retrieved Population]]. Completeness testing reconciles the retrieved extract to this intended set ([[Population Completeness]], [[Data Reconciliation]]).
