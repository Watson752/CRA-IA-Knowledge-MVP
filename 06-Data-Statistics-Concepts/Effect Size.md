**Effect size** describes the magnitude of a difference or association (absolute or relative), separate from whether it is statistically detectable. Large samples can yield tiny but “significant” effects; small samples can miss large effects.

Use effect size with [[Materiality]] and [[Operational Significance]] when deciding whether to investigate or report. Pair with [[Confidence Interval]]s where design allows.
