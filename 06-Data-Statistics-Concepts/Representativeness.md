**Representativeness** means the analysed set (frame or sample) reflects the [[Intended Population]] well enough for the inference being drawn. Statistical designs ([[Random Sampling]], [[Stratified Sampling]]) aim at representativeness when the [[Sampling Frame]] is complete.

[[Judgmental Sampling]] and convenience picks are typically **not** representative for population-rate claims. Incomplete frames, [[Selection Bias]], and [[Survivorship Bias]] destroy representativeness even when selection within the retrieved set looks orderly.
