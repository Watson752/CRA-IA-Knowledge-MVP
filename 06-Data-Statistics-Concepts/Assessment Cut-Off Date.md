An **assessment cut-off date** is the point in time through which transactions or records are included in an assessment, audit test, or statistical estimate. Events after cut-off belong to subsequent periods. Cut-off is fundamental to fairness of [[Performance Reporting]], tax or benefit processing reviews, and financial-style testing.

Misapplied cut-off causes [[Population Completeness]] issues and distorts trends when compared across periods. Auditors perform cut-off tests near period-end to verify recording in correct periods.

In dynamic systems, late-arriving data may require [[Reassessment Data]] or [[Statistical Revision]]. Reports should state cut-off explicitly so readers do not assume real-time completeness.
