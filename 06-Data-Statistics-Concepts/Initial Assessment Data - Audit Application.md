**Initial assessment data** is the first snapshot of records or metrics used for a decision, audit fieldwork start, or preliminary statistical release, taken at or before an [[Assessment Cut-Off Date]]. It reflects information available at that moment and may omit late corrections.

Initial data supports timeliness; [[Reassessment Data]] may follow when updates arrive. Users must not treat initial figures as final if the process anticipates revision ([[Statistical Revision]]).

Auditors document whether workpapers used initial or updated extracts and whether differences materially affect [[Finding]]s. [[Data Quality]] at initial capture still matters for downstream rework cost.

Contrast with ad hoc drafts lacking governance— initial assessment should follow defined extraction rules.
