**Systematic exclusion** omits a class of in-scope items by filter, join, status rule, channel, region, or period—producing biased coverage of the [[Intended Population]]. A form of [[Missing Data]] / omitted segments that threatens [[Population Completeness]] and can induce [[Selection Bias]].

Document intended [[Inclusion and Exclusion Rules]]; quantify silent pipeline filters ([[Data Transformation]], [[Rejected Records]]).
