**Judgmental sampling** (directed / purposeful selection) deliberately chooses items using auditor knowledge—unusual amounts, high-risk segments, anomalies from [[Outlier Analysis]], or material items ([[Materiality]], [[Risk-Based Selection]]).

**Value:** efficient for investigating known risks and unusual items; complements statistical samples.

**Critical limit:** judgmental (and convenience) samples are **not** designed for statistical [[Representativeness]]. They normally **do not** support [[Statistical Extrapolation]] to the entire [[Intended Population]]. Projecting error rates as if the sample were random overstates assurance ([[Sampling Risk]]).

Distinct from [[Professional Judgment]] (engagement decisions) and from [[Stratified Sampling]] (partition then sample within strata).
