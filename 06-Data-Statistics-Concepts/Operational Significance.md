**Operational significance** (practical importance) asks whether a result matters materially to program outcomes, compliance, service delivery, financial exposure, security, fairness, workload, or management decision-making.

Distinct from [[Statistical Significance]]. A detectable difference may be operationally trivial; a rare event may be operationally critical without a stable significance test ([[Rare High-Impact Events]], [[Materiality]], [[Consequence or Impact]]).

Elevate [[Finding]]s using [[Criteria]], [[Professional Judgment]], and impact—not p-values alone. “Significant” in [[Evidence Evaluation]] / [[Finding]] means **audit-report** significance, not a statistical test result.
