**Duplicate analytical work** occurs when multiple teams independently build similar datasets, reports, or models— wasting effort and producing conflicting numbers. Duplication often signals weak [[Business Intelligence Governance]], unclear [[Roles and Responsibilities]], or lack of [[Horizontal Collaboration]].

Duplication increases [[Risk]] that decisions rely on non-certified [[Outdated Analytics]]. Central platforms, reusable data products, and mandate to reuse official metrics reduce duplication.

Auditors may note parallel reporting chains when testing [[Performance Reporting]] consistency. Recommendations often steer toward shared definitions under [[Data Governance]] rather than banning local analysis entirely.

Some duplication is healthy for assurance (independent verification) if roles and methods are explicit.
