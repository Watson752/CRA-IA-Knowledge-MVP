**Outdated analytics** are reports, scripts, models, or dashboards that no longer reflect current data definitions, systems, regulations, or business rules— yet remain in circulation. They create false confidence and conflict with governed [[Business Intelligence]] products.

Causes include staff turnover without [[Knowledge Continuity]], absent version control, and [[Siloed Knowledge]]. Symptoms: reconciliations that never foot, deprecated fields, or schedules that silently fail.

Governance responses: inventory of analytical assets, ownership, periodic recertification, and decommissioning standards. [[Continuous Improvement]] and [[Business Intelligence Governance]] provide forums to retire stale content.

Auditors flag reliance on uncertified analytics when conclusions depend on them; prefer governed sources with [[Evidence Reliability]] documentation.
