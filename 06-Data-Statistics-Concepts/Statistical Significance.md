**Statistical significance** asks whether an observed difference or relationship would be unlikely under a specified statistical model or assumption. It is about **detectability under a model**, not automatic business importance.

This vault does **not** mandate a universal significance level (e.g. p < 0.05) as CRA or engagement policy. Report-bound confidence language in public cases (e.g. Audit Yield **95%** for a **specified segment**) must not be generalized.

In very large datasets, tiny differences can become statistically detectable while remaining negligible for [[Operational Significance]] and [[Materiality]]. Conversely, non-significance does **not** prove absence of risk—especially for [[Rare High-Impact Events]] or small samples ([[Small-Cell Analysis]]).

Prefer [[Confidence Interval]]s and [[Effect Size]] plus [[Professional Judgment]] over a binary significant/non-significant label when wording [[Finding]]s or an [[Audit Conclusion]].
