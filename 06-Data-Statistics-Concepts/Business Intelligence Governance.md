**Business intelligence governance** defines roles, standards, and processes for official metrics, dashboards, and semantic models. It answers who may publish data products, how definitions are approved, how changes are versioned, and how access is controlled. Strong BI governance reduces conflicting KPIs and supports [[Evidence-Based Decision-Making]].

Elements often include a metric registry, data stewardship, promotion workflows from sandbox to production, and alignment with [[Information and Data Strategy]]. [[Chief Data Officer]] functions frequently sponsor or chair BI governance forums.

Auditors review whether reported performance in audits matches “certified” BI definitions and whether ad hoc reports drove decisions without control. Weak governance contributes to [[Outdated Analytics]] and misstated [[Performance Reporting]].

BI governance sits within broader [[Data Governance]] and [[IT Controls]] (access, change management).
