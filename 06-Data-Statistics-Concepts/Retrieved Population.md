The **retrieved population** is the set of items actually obtained for analysis—extracts, registers, query results, or reports. It may be smaller than the [[Intended Population]] because of filters, rejects, cut-offs, matching failures, or access limits ([[Missing Data]], [[Rejected Records]]).

[[Full-Population Analysis]] of the retrieved set still requires stating when conclusions apply only to that subset ([[Population Completeness]]).
