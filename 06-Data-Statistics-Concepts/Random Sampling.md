**Random sampling** selects units from a complete [[Sampling Frame]] so each eligible unit has a known (typically equal) chance of selection, without auditor preference for particular items.

**Assumptions:** frame matches the [[Intended Population]] ([[Population Completeness]]); selection is free of convenience bias. Incomplete frames make even “random-looking” picks non-representative ([[Sampling Risk]]).

**Conclusions:** may support [[Statistical Extrapolation]] / projection when design, size, and evaluation allow—disclose in reporting. Does not remove [[Data Quality]] limits.

**Limitations:** rare high-risk items may be undersampled unless combined with [[Stratified Sampling]] or [[Risk-Based Selection]]; no universal sample-size formula in this vault ([[Control Frequency]] still requires enough instances across the [[Audit Period]]).
