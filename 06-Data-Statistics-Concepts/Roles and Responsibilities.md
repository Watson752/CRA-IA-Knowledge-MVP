**Roles and responsibilities** clarify who is accountable, responsible, consulted, and informed for activities— including [[Control Ownership]], data stewardship, audit response, and analytics publication. Ambiguity leads to control gaps, delayed remediation, and [[Siloed Knowledge]].

Frameworks such as RACI matrices document decisions for complex processes. The [[Three Lines Model]] assigns distinct roles to operational management, oversight functions, and internal audit. [[Chief Data Officer]] and IT security roles exemplify second-line specialization.

Clear roles support [[Governance]], [[Risk Management]], and effective [[Follow-up]] on [[Recommendation]]s. Auditors test whether documented roles match practice (e.g., who approves access, who signs reports).

Enterprise [[Information and Data Strategy]] should align role definitions with actual staffing and committees.
