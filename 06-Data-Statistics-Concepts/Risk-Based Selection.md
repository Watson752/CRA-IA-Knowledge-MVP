**Risk-based selection** prioritises items using [[Risk Assessment]] signals—high value, high risk, anomaly scores, or [[Rare High-Impact Events]]—often as [[Judgmental Sampling]] or as a certainty stratum inside [[Stratified Sampling]].

Use to focus limited fieldwork. Do **not** treat risk-based picks alone as statistically representative of the whole population ([[Representativeness]], [[Statistical Extrapolation]]). Pair with random/stratified coverage when population rates are needed.
