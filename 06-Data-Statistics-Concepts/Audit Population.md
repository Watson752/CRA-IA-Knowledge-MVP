The **audit population** is the set of items (records, transactions, accounts, cases, or events) about which the engagement intends to draw conclusions for the [[Audit Objective]] within [[Scope]] and [[Audit Period]] against [[Criteria]].

Keep layers distinct:

| Layer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [[Intended Population]] | All items that belong given objective, scope, period, criteria, and [[Inclusion and Exclusion Rules]] |
| [[Retrieved Population]] | Items actually obtained in extracts, registers, or reports |
| [[Sampling Frame]] | List/dataset from which a sample is drawn—should match the intended population |
| Sample | Items selected for testing ([[Sample Selection]]) |

Do **not** treat a received dataset as the audit population until [[Population Completeness]] is assessed. [[Transactional Dataset|Transactional datasets]] are common inputs; they are not automatically the intended population.
