**Statistical extrapolation** (projection) extends sample results to a broader population or frame using a statistical design and stated evaluation method. Supported only when selection was statistical (e.g. [[Random Sampling]], [[Stratified Sampling]] on a sound [[Sampling Frame]]) and assumptions hold.

**Unsupported** for [[Judgmental Sampling]], convenience samples, or biased frames—those support item-level or scoped conclusions only ([[Sampling Risk]]). Always disclose whether results were projected.

Do not treat Audit Yield’s report-bound confidence statement as a universal extrapolation rule for all engagements.
