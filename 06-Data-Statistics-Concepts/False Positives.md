A **false positive** is a rule/alert that fires when it should not (relative to policy intent). High false-positive rates drive legitimate [[Manual Overrides]] and alert fatigue; ignoring them leaves bad rules in place.

**Official EFMS theme:** some rules generated many false-positive alerts; MAP included review of high false-positive rules ([[Internal Audit - Enterprise Fraud Management System]]). Alert counts alone are insufficient evidence ([[System-Generated Evidence]]).
