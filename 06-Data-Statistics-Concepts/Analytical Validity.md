**Analytical validity** means the method, population, assumptions, and interpretation appropriately answer the audit question. Distinct from [[Reproducibility]]:

```text
Reproducible → same inputs + method → same result
Valid → result is fit to support the conclusion for the objective/criteria
```

Invalid-but-reproducible examples: complete analysis of the wrong [[Retrieved Population]]; reproducible association misread as causation; reproducible filter that silently applies [[Systematic Exclusion]].

Judge validity with [[Population Completeness]], [[Data Quality]], [[Criteria]], [[Professional Judgment]], and [[How Statistical Limitations Affect Audit Conclusions]].
