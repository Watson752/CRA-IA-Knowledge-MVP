**Reassessment data** updates or replaces [[Initial Assessment Data]] after new information, corrections, appeals outcomes, or extended cut-off windows. Reassessment improves accuracy but may change prior conclusions, rankings, or risk scores.

Processes should define triggers for reassessment, roles, and communication to stakeholders who acted on initial figures. Without controls, dual versions circulate and undermine [[Evidence-Based Decision-Making]].

Auditors evaluate whether reassessment controls prevent unauthorized retroactive changes and whether logs support [[Evidence Reliability]]. Performance and compliance reviews may test sample cases through initial and final states.

Reassessment relates to [[Statistical Revision]] in published aggregates and to [[Continuous Improvement]] in operational metrics.
