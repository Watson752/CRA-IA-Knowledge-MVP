An **information and data strategy** articulates how an organization will create, manage, share, and use data and information to achieve [[Enterprise Objectives]]. It typically covers vision, principles, target capabilities, priority use cases, governance, and investment roadmaps— aligned with [[Governance]] and [[Risk Management]].

Strategy connects [[Data Governance]], [[Business Intelligence]], [[Analytics]], [[Cybersecurity]], and architecture choices. Without strategy, tools proliferate via ad hoc [[Tool Acquisition]] and [[Duplicate Analytical Work]] increases.

The [[Chief Data Officer]] or equivalent often leads development and monitoring of the strategy. Auditors may assess whether documented strategy matches observed funding, skills, and outcomes— as general governance themes in engagements.

Official departmental data strategies may be cited from public plans when source notes exist; this concept note stays generic.
