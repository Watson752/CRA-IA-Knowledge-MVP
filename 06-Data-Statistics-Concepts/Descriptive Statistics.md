**Descriptive statistics** summarise what happened in a dataset—counts, rates, distributions, concentrations, and simple time profiles—without claiming causal inference. First step in [[Analytics]] profiling for [[Outlier Analysis]], [[Trend Analysis]], and [[Full-Population Analysis]].

Document the [[Retrieved Population]], [[Inclusion and Exclusion Rules]], and limitations ([[Reproducibility]]). Large-N descriptions can still be operationally trivial ([[Operational Significance]], [[Effect Size]]).
