**Comparability across editions** means users can meaningfully compare reports, tables, or indicators over time or between publications. Breaks in comparability arise from definition changes, classification updates, [[Statistical Revision]], [[Rounding]] rule changes, or scope changes in [[Performance Reporting]].

Publishers may provide bridged series, parallel publication of old and new definitions, or marked break points. Without such aids, trend analysis misleads decision-makers and auditors.

When audits benchmark performance year-over-year, verify that KPI definitions and populations remained stable or that adjustments were documented. [[Data Suppression]] changes can also alter apparent trends.

[[Analytics]] projects should store edition metadata alongside datasets to prevent mixing incompatible vintages.
