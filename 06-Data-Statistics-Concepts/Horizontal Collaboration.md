**Horizontal collaboration** is cooperation across organizational units at similar hierarchy levels— sharing data, methods, and lessons without relying solely on vertical command chains. It improves consistency of [[Analytics]], reduces [[Duplicate Analytical Work]], and spreads good practices for [[Data Governance]] and [[Business Intelligence Governance]].

Enablers include communities of practice, shared platforms, clear [[Roles and Responsibilities]], and psychological safety to ask questions. Barriers include competing priorities, funding silos, and [[Siloed Knowledge]] in local files.

Auditors may observe whether risk and performance insights cross functions or remain trapped in single branches. [[Three Lines Model]] coordination between first and second lines also depends on horizontal habits.

Horizontal collaboration supports [[Continuous Improvement]] and [[Evidence-Based Decision-Making]] at enterprise scale.
