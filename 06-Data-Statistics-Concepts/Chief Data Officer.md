The **chief data officer** (CDO) is a senior role accountable for enterprise data strategy, [[Data Governance]], and value realization from data assets— often partnering with CIO, CISO, and program leaders. Responsibilities may include standards, quality programs, metadata, analytics priorities, and culture for [[Evidence-Based Decision-Making]].

CDO authority varies: some organizations grant budget and policy power; others are coordinating roles. Success requires clear [[Roles and Responsibilities]] relative to IT operations and security.

Auditors examine whether governance forums exist, issues escalate, and [[Information and Data Strategy]] is operational—not only whether a CDO title is filled.

Public sector appointments may be described in official org charts when sourced; no specific incumbent claims appear in this note.

## CRA-specific official placement (not this note’s general definition)

On the CRA Organization baseline, the Assistant Commissioner of [[Service, Innovation, and Integration Branch|SIIB]] is also the Chief Data Officer (and Chief Service Officer). That is an **official public CRA organizational fact** on the branch/org notes — distinct from the general CDO concept above. Enterprise CDO placement does **not** make SIIB the data owner of every program dataset. See [[Data Owner]] and [[Ownership and Assurance Roles]].
