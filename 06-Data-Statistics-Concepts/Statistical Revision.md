**Statistical revision** is the practice of updating previously published estimates when new data, methodology improvements, or error corrections become available. Revisions can shift levels and growth rates; agencies often publish revision policies and vintage charts for transparency.

Revisions differ from routine [[Reassessment Data]] at operational level but share communication challenges. Users comparing time series must align vintages or use consistently revised history ([[Comparability Across Editions]]).

Auditors citing public statistics should note publication date, revision status, and known limitations in [[How Statistical Limitations Affect Audit Conclusions]]. Treating preliminary releases as final can overstate precision.

Major methodological changes require extra documentation and stakeholder notice.
