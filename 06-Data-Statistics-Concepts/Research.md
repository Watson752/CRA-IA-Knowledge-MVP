**Research** is structured inquiry to produce reliable knowledge— defining questions, methods, data collection, analysis, and conclusions. In government and audit contexts, research supports program evaluation, policy analysis, and exploratory assurance work distinct from routine [[Analytics]] or [[Business Intelligence]] reporting.

Research designs include surveys, experiments, quasi-experiments, case studies, and mixed methods. Standards emphasize ethics, privacy, reproducibility, and clear limitations. Findings should state uncertainty and avoid overgeneralization from small or biased samples.

[[Internal Audit and Program Evaluation]] functions may use research methods for effectiveness questions while still meeting professional assurance standards where applicable. [[Evidence]] from research must meet [[Evidence Reliability]] expectations when cited in [[Finding]]s.

Research outputs feed [[Evidence-Based Decision-Making]] when communicated with context and caveats.
