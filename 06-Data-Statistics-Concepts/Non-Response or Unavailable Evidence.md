**Non-response or unavailable evidence** is expected supporting information—documents, approvals, logs, or workpapers—that cannot be obtained. Listed in [[Missing Data]] as missing supporting evidence. Distinct from missing analytic fields/records, though all can reduce [[Evidence Reliability]] and force narrower [[Audit Conclusion]]s.

Treat intentional redaction/[[Data Suppression]] differently from accidental gaps; both constrain conclusions.
