**Continuous improvement** is the ongoing cycle of measuring performance, identifying gaps, implementing changes, and verifying results— Plan-Do-Check-Act and related models. In audit contexts, it applies to remediation ([[Management Action Plan]]), audit methodology refinement, and organizational processes under review.

For data and analytics functions, improvement means raising [[Data Quality]], retiring [[Outdated Analytics]], and learning from [[Finding]]s and [[Follow-up]]. Without feedback loops, the same exceptions recur.

Improvement should align with [[Enterprise Objectives]] and be evidenced—not merely aspirational slogans. [[Performance Reporting]] and [[Monitoring and Reporting]] supply metrics for improvement cycles.

Auditors distinguish one-off fixes from sustained capability building when evaluating management responses.
