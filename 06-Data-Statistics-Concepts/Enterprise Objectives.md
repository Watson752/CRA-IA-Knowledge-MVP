**Enterprise objectives** are organization-wide outcomes and priorities— strategic goals, service standards, compliance mandates, and resource stewardship targets. They cascade to programs and metrics used in [[Performance Reporting]] and [[Business Intelligence]]. Clear objectives enable [[Risk Management]] and meaningful [[Criteria]] in audits.

Objectives should be measurable or have observable indicators where possible. Conflicting objectives (e.g., speed versus accuracy) require explicit trade-off governance. [[Information and Data Strategy]] supports objectives with data capabilities; [[Evidence-Based Decision-Making]] depends on aligned metrics.

Auditors map [[Audit Objective]] and scope to enterprise objectives to assess relevance and significance of [[Finding]]s. Changes in corporate plans may shift risk focus across audit cycles.

Public organizations often express objectives in departmental plans and results reports— to be linked via official source notes when available.
