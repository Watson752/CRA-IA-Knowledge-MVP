The **sampling frame** is the list or dataset from which sample units are drawn ([[Sample Selection]]). For statistical designs, the frame should correspond to the [[Intended Population]]. Gaps between frame and intended population create [[Selection Bias]] and inflate [[Sampling Risk]] even when selection within the frame looks random.

Validate the frame with [[Population Completeness]] and [[Data Reconciliation]] before selecting items—or document a scope limitation.
