# Electronic Filing Statistics

This note records **operational highlights** for individual return filing from CRA’s public **Departmental Plan 2026–27**, not assessment-based tables from [[Individual Income Tax Return Statistics]].

## Actual results — fiscal year 2024–25

Under **“CRA by the numbers in 2024–25”**, CRA states (actual period **2024–25**):

- Taxpayers filed **more than 33.2 million** individual income tax and benefit returns in 2024–25.
- **92%** of those returns were filed **digitally**.

These figures describe **filing and processing activity** in the stated fiscal year. They must not be read as counts from a T1 statistical cut-off ([[Assessment Cut-Off Date]]) or reconciled to Table 1 return counts without explicit methodology.
