# Statistical Revision

CRA states that **all statistics** in **Individual Income Tax Return Statistics** are **subject to revision**.

## Drivers of revision

Revisions can arise when:

- Additional returns are **processed** after an edition’s [[Assessment Cut-Off Date]].
- **Reassessments** update line values ([[Reassessment Data]]).
- CRA republishes an edition or releases a new edition for the same tax year with an extended cut-off ([[Comparability Across Editions]]).
- Methodological or classification updates are documented in explanatory notes (for example new tax credits or line items for a tax year).

Open Government dataset metadata may show a **resource update date** distinct from the tax year; users should cite the edition URL and access date.

## Audit documentation

When citing a published CRA table in workpapers, record **edition year**, **tax year**, **cut-off date**, and **access date** so revision is traceable.
