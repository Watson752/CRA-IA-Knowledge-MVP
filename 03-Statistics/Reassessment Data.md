# Reassessment Data

**Individual Income Tax Return Statistics** incorporate **reassessments** completed on or before the [[Assessment Cut-Off Date]] for the stated tax year.

## Official rule

For returns that have been **reassessed**, CRA uses the **most current reassessed values** to prepare the tables. The **most recent assessment** (whether initial or reassessed) drives published aggregates.

## Coverage window

Statistics contain the most recent assessments or reassessments for the tax year **up to the edition’s cut-off date** (for the 2023 tax year 2025 edition: **November 30, 2024**). Reassessments finalized **after** that date are excluded until a subsequent edition or [[Statistical Revision]].

## Analytical caution

Aggregate tables mix initial and reassessed outcomes without filer-level flags. Comparisons to audit **reassessment yield** or collections timelines (see evaluation themes in [[99-Sources/source-notes/SRC-CRA-Eval-Audit-Yield-2020]]) require different data sources and periods.
