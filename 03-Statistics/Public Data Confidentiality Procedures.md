# Public Data Confidentiality Procedures

For **Individual Income Tax Return Statistics**, CRA applies confidentiality procedures **to ensure the protection of tax filer information**.

## Published rules (2023 tax year edition)

Official explanatory notes state:

- Data have been **suppressed where warranted** (see [[Data Suppression]]).
- **Counts** are rounded to the nearest multiple of **10** ([[Rounding]]).
- **Dollar amounts** in Tables 1 to 5 are rounded to the nearest **thousand**.
- **Totals may not add** due to rounding or suppression.
- A displayed **zero [0]** indicates information **suppressed for confidentiality**; suppressed cells can include **valid zeroes**.

Table footnotes direct readers to these procedures when row or column totals do not match summed cells.

## Relationship to general concepts

Profession-wide disclosure-control concepts are available in [[Data Suppression — Audit Application]]. This note records **CRA’s published implementation** for the T1 aggregate statistics product.

## Audit relevance

When public CRA tables are used as [[Evidence]], auditors should treat suppressed or rounded cells as limiting precision for [[Small-Cell Analysis]] and for [[How Statistical Limitations Affect Audit Conclusions]].
