# Assessment Cut-Off Date

**Individual Income Tax Return Statistics** include assessments and reassessments processed only up to a stated **cut-off date**. Returns assessed or reassessed **after** that date are excluded until a later edition or [[Statistical Revision]].

## 2025 edition — 2023 tax year

For the edition covering the **2023 tax year**:

- Statistics contain the most recent 2023 tax year **assessments or reassessments** up to **November 30, 2024**.
- The **2025 edition** presents data based on 2023 tax year returns **filed and processed** up to **November 30, 2024**.

## Timeliness change

Starting with this edition, CRA uses an **earlier extraction cut-off** (**November 30, 2024**) instead of **June 30, 2025** (prior approach referenced on the official page) to **increase timeliness**. When comparing this edition to earlier ones, **differences in extraction time frames** must be considered ([[Comparability Across Editions]]).

## Relationship to filing statistics

Operational filing volumes (for example [[Electronic Filing Statistics]] for fiscal year **2024–25**) use different reporting periods and definitions than this assessment cut-off.
