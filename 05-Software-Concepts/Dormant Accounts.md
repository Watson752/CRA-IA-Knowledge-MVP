**Dormant accounts** are enabled identities with no recent authentic use (or no human owner), yet still able to authenticate. They increase [[Unauthorized Access]] and [[Excessive Privileges]] risk. Distinct from “dormant controls” in [[Security Controls]] (unused *controls*, not unused *accounts*).

Controls: inactivity disablement, owner attestation in [[Periodic Access Review]], monitoring of reactivation. Include dormant IDs in [[User Access Dataset]] populations.
