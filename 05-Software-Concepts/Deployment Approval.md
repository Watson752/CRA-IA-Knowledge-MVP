**Deployment approval** (deployment authority) is authorization to release a change into production (or controlled pilot)—distinct from [[Change Approval]] of intent and from the engineer who builds the change. Links [[Tool Deployment]] and [[Control Implementation]].

Pre-production test evidence supports implementation readiness but does **not** prove [[Operating Effectiveness]] throughout the [[Audit Period]].
