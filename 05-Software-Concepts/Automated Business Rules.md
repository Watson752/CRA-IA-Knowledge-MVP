**Automated business rules** encode policy or risk criteria in software (validations, eligibility checks, detection models, workflow gates). Chain: business requirement → rule definition → technical implementation / [[System Configuration]] → [[Deployment Approval|deployment]] → operation → exceptions/overrides → monitoring.

A type of [[Automated Control]]. **Official EFMS theme:** business rules identify questionable activity; rule-change governance and false-positive alerts were public improvement areas ([[Internal Audit - Enterprise Fraud Management System]]). Do not reconstruct protected rule logic.
