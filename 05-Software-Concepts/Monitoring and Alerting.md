**Monitoring and alerting** uses rules/thresholds on logs and telemetry to raise alerts for triage—**downstream of log generation**, not a substitute for it. Distinct from periodic [[Log Review]] and from management [[Exception Report Review]].

[[Monitoring and Reporting]] remains the broader operational/security monitoring concept; this note emphasizes the alert path. EFMS publicly describes real-time business-rule alerting on audit-trail activity ([[Internal Audit - Enterprise Fraud Management System]]); investigation stages may be out of published scope.
