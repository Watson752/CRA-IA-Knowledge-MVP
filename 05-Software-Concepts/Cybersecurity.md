**Cybersecurity** is the protection of information systems, data, and services from unauthorized access, disruption, modification, or destruction. It spans people, process, and technology: identity management, network security, application security, vulnerability management, incident response, and security awareness. Cyber risk is a subset of enterprise [[Risk]] with high impact potential for confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

Organizations typically align programs to frameworks such as the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover) or equivalent national guidance. [[Defence in Depth]] and [[Security Controls]] operationalize strategy. [[Monitoring and Reporting]] provides visibility into threats and control effectiveness.

Auditors examine governance, policy, control design, and operating effectiveness—not merely the presence of tools. [[IT Controls]] map closely to cybersecurity expectations in modern enterprises. Cybersecurity intersects [[Data Governance]] and [[Artificial Intelligence]] as data and models become critical assets.
