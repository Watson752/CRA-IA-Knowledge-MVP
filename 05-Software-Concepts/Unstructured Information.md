**Unstructured information** lacks a fixed schema: correspondence, policy PDFs, scanned forms, chat logs, web content, and multimedia. It carries substantial operational and legal value but is harder to search, classify, and analyze at scale than [[Structured Data]]. Retention, privacy, and discovery obligations still apply.

Organizations manage unstructured content through document management systems, records classification, and search tools. [[Artificial Intelligence]] and modern search can extract entities and themes but introduce validation needs— outputs are not automatic [[Evidence]] without corroboration.

Auditors review whether critical controls (approvals, contracts, security exceptions) are documented in retrievable form and whether retention policies are enforced. [[Siloed Knowledge]] in personal drives or mailboxes is a recurring risk theme.

Linking unstructured sources to structured transactions (metadata, case IDs) improves traceability for investigations and audits.
