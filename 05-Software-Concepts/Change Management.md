**Change management** governs initiation, assessment, authorization, build/test, deployment, and post-implementation review of changes to systems, configurations, rules, and pipelines. Teaching chain: [[Change Requester]] → [[Change Approval]] → [[Code Review]] / build → [[Deployment Approval]] → [[Monitoring and Reporting|post-implementation monitoring]].

Distinct from transactional [[Manual Overrides]]. Weak change history undermines reliance on [[Automated Control]]s ([[IT Controls]], [[Tool Deployment]]). **EFMS official theme:** ad hoc business-rule changes and incomplete central change history ([[Internal Audit - Enterprise Fraud Management System]]).
