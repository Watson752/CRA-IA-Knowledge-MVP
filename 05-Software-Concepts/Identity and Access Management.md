**Identity and access management (IAM)** is the set of processes and technology that identify users and services, authenticate them, authorize access, and manage the lifecycle of identities (joiners, movers, leavers). IAM underpins least privilege and [[Role-Based Access Control]].

Auditors examine whether identities are unique and attributable, whether privileges match duties, and whether joiner–mover–leaver processes prevent orphan or excessive access ([[Privileged Access]], [[Access Approval]], [[Periodic Access Review]]).

Do **not** invent CRA-specific IAM product names or claim current CRA access-control weakness without a public source.
