**Security controls** are safeguards prescribed to meet security [[Criteria]] and reduce [[Risk]] to information assets. They include administrative controls (policies, training), technical controls (firewalls, MFA, encryption), and physical controls (badges, secure facilities). Frameworks such as NIST SP 800-53 and ISO 27001 annexes provide structured control baselines that organizations tailor via risk assessment.

Controls are selected based on asset value, threats, vulnerabilities, and regulatory obligations. Implementation requires [[Control Ownership]], configuration management, and periodic testing. Compensating controls may address gaps when primary controls cannot be deployed immediately.

Auditors map controls to risks and evaluate design and operation. Overlapping controls may indicate [[Defence in Depth]]; missing or dormant controls (e.g., logging not reviewed) are common review themes. Security controls intersect [[IT Controls]] and broader [[Governance]].

Cloud and SaaS environments require explicit shared-responsibility clarity— which party implements which controls.
