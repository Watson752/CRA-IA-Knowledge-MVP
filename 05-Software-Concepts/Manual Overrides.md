**Manual overrides** let a person bypass or force an outcome contrary to an [[Automated Business Rules|automated business rule]]. **Overrides may be legitimate** (false-positive correction, documented exception policy, emergency service) but require governance: who may override, when, why, whose approval, logging, and later review.

Risk is **uncontrolled** or [[Unmonitored Manual Overrides|unmonitored]] override use—not the mere existence of an override path. Do **not** assume every override is improper. Do **not** invent CRA override UIs; EFMS public scope is detection alerting, not a published transactional-override audit.
