**Inadequate segregation of duties** is the risk state when incompatible privileges or duties are concentrated so errors or misuse can occur and be concealed. Detect via SoD conflict reports over [[User Access Dataset]] populations; remediate by splitting roles, compensating reviews, or removing standing privilege.
