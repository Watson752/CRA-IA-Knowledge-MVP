**Business intelligence tools** are software platforms for querying, visualizing, and distributing data-driven insights— dashboards, semantic layers, self-service reporting, and sometimes embedded [[Analytics]]. Examples include commercial and open-source stacks (reporting servers, visualization clients, data warehouses). Tools consume [[Structured Data]] from curated models or live connections.

Governance matters: without [[Business Intelligence Governance]], divergent definitions (“multiple versions of truth”) undermine [[Evidence-Based Decision-Making]]. Access controls, certified datasets, and change management are [[IT Controls]] concerns.

Auditors evaluate data lineage into BI models, who can publish content, and whether public or regulatory [[Performance Reporting]] matches internal dashboards. [[Outdated Analytics]] and [[Duplicate Analytical Work]] often stem from tool sprawl.

Tool lifecycle ties to [[Tool Acquisition]] and [[Tool Deployment]] with security and privacy review.
