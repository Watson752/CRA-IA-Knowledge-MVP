**Incorrect automated decisions** occur when rule outcomes diverge from approved policy intent—via bad design, wrong configuration, stale reference data, or unmonitored overrides. Analyze with [[False Positives]], [[False Negatives]], and outcome monitoring—not alert volume alone ([[System-Generated Evidence]]).
