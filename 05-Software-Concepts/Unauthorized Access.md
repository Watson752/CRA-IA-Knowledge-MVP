**Unauthorized access** is access or use without valid authorization—never approved, revoked but still active, credential misuse, or policy-prohibited use. Distinct from [[Excessive Privileges]] (authorized but too broad).

**Official public CRA context (EFMS):** [[Internal Audit - Enterprise Fraud Management System]] states EFMS was implemented to reduce risks of unauthorized employee access to taxpayer information and uses audit trails and business rules to identify questionable activity. That is a **detective monitoring** case theme—not a public finding that CRA privileged-access provisioning is weak.
