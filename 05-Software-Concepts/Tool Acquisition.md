**Tool acquisition** is the process of selecting and procuring software, platforms, or technology services to meet business needs. It typically includes requirements definition, market research, security and privacy assessment, contract negotiation, licensing, and alignment with enterprise architecture and [[Information and Data Strategy]]. Public sector acquisition often follows formal procurement rules and fairness principles.

Risk areas include vendor lock-in, unclear data residency, inadequate SLAs, and shadow IT when acquisition bypasses governance. Security reviews should map vendor controls to organizational [[Criteria]] before [[Tool Deployment]].

Auditors examine whether acquisition documented alternatives, involved [[Cybersecurity]] and legal review, and established ownership for ongoing [[Monitoring and Reporting]] and patch management.

Acquisition connects to [[Business Intelligence Tools]] and [[Artificial Intelligence]] when organizations buy analytics or model-hosting capabilities.
