**Excessive privileges** exist when access was granted (or left after a role change) beyond what the duty requires. The login may still be “authorized,” but the entitlement is inappropriate. Distinct from [[Unauthorized Access]] (no valid authorization / prohibited use).

Common causes: privilege creep, shared admin rights, standing elevation, and incomplete leaver/mover processes. Controls: least privilege / [[Role-Based Access Control]], [[Access Approval]], [[Periodic Access Review]], and timely revocation.

[[Tool Deployment]] notes excessive privileges as a deployment risk.
