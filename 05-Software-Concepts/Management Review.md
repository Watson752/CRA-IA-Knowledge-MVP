**Management review** is the detective challenge of final reports/dashboards before decision reliance—variance explanation, known limitations, and reviewer independence from the preparer where required. Distinct from [[Management Reporting]] (producing the report), from real-time [[Monitoring and Alerting]], and from AERB assurance.

OE evidence: completed reviews, exception follow-up, sign-off **plus** substance (inquiry/sign-off alone is weak—[[Operating Effectiveness]], [[Document Review]]).
