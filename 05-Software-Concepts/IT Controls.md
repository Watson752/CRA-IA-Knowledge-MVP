**IT controls** are [[Control]]s over information technology general controls (ITGCs) and application controls that support reliable processing, security, and change management. ITGCs commonly include access management, change/release management, backup and recovery, job scheduling, and segregation of duties in administrative functions. Application controls enforce business rules at transaction level (validations, approvals, reconciliations).

Weak ITGCs can undermine many application controls simultaneously— a central theme in IT audit. COBIT and similar frameworks organize IT governance and management objectives; [[Security Controls]] address confidentiality, integrity, and availability specifically.

Auditors often test ITGCs before relying on automated application controls or [[Analytics]] over [[Structured Data]]. [[Tool Deployment]] and [[Tool Acquisition]] processes are evaluated for governance and security integration.

IT controls connect to [[Three Lines Model]] roles: operations runs controls, IT security and enterprise architecture provide second-line guidance, internal audit provides assurance.
