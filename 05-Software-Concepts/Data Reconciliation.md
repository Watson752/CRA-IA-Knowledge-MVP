**Data reconciliation** compares counts/amounts/hashes between pipeline stages or to independent registers. Detective control for completeness breaks—**not** proof of field-level [[Data Accuracy]]. Document breaks and clearance ([[Manual Control]], [[Missing Data]] procedures).
