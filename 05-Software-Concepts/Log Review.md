**Log review** is the detective control (manual or semi-automated) that examines log extracts or exception reports for anomalies—**distinct from log generation**. Generation without review is a common gap ([[Security Controls]] “dormant controls” theme for unused monitoring).

OE evidence: review schedules, reviewer identity, exceptions followed up ([[Manual Control]], [[Operating Effectiveness]]). Related to but not identical with real-time [[Monitoring and Alerting]].
