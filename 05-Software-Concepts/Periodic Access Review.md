**Periodic access review** (access certification) is a **detective** control: on a defined frequency, an appropriate reviewer examines whether retained access remains necessary and documents keep/remove decisions. Distinct from [[Access Approval]] at grant time.

[[Recommendation]] uses “quarterly access reviews for privileged accounts with documented sign-off” as a measurable-recommendation example. **Documented sign-off is not sufficient** without evidence that entitlements were examined and that remove decisions were executed ([[Operating Effectiveness]], [[Document Review]], [[Access Review Testing]]). [[Control Ownership]] notes reviews lapse without ownership. Population must be complete ([[User Access Dataset]], [[Population Completeness]]); include [[Joiner-Mover-Leaver]] outcomes.
