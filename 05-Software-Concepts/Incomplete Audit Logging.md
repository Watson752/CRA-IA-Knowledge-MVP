**Incomplete audit logging** is the failure mode when required events, fields, periods, or feeds are missing—so traceability and [[Evidence Reliability]] collapse. Causes include filters, failed loads, disabled logging, truncated retention, or missing identity/timestamp fields ([[Missing Data]], [[Population Completeness]]).

Do not treat system-generated logs as complete without reconciliation to independent records ([[System-Generated Evidence]]).
