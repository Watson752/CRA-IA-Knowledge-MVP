**Role-based access control (RBAC)** grants permissions through defined roles aligned to job duties, rather than ad hoc individual entitlements. Good RBAC supports least privilege and makes [[Periodic Access Review]] tractable.

Auditors compare role definitions to business responsibilities ([[Roles and Responsibilities]]), test for toxic combinations ([[Segregation of Duties]]), and sample whether users hold only needed roles.
