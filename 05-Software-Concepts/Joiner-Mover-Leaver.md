**Joiner–mover–leaver (JML)** is the identity lifecycle that grants, adjusts, and removes access as people join, change roles, or leave. Incomplete mover/leaver steps drive [[Excessive Privileges]], [[Dormant Accounts]], and orphan entitlements. Pair access listings with employment/role information when testing ([[Access Review Testing]], [[Identity and Access Management]]).

Do **not** invent CRA HR system names.
