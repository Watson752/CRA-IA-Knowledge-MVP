**Automated eligibility validation** applies policy criteria to determine whether a case, claim, or transaction qualifies. Requires clear approved requirements and tested exception paths. Incorrect automated decisions may be [[False Positives]] / [[False Negatives]] relative to policy intent ([[Incorrect Automated Decisions]]).
