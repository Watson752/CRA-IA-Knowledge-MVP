**Access approval** is the **preventive** control that authorizes a grant or change of access **before** (or at) entitlement. Distinct from [[Periodic Access Review]] (detective, recurring after grant).

Evidence: request tickets, approver identity/authority, business need, role requested. Approver should not be the sole implementer where SoD requires separation ([[Segregation of Duties]], [[Manual Control]]).
