**Management reporting** produces internal decision reports/dashboards from curated datasets. Related to [[Performance Reporting]] and [[Business Intelligence]] but focused on reliance conditions for auditors: lineage, quality, cut-off, calc logic, access, change control, and [[Management Review]].

Do not treat reports as reliable merely because they are system-generated ([[System-Generated Evidence]]). Multi-source measures need matching/completeness analysis ([[Evaluation - Audit Yield]]). Business/report ownership remains with the process/program owner—not automatically with [[Technical Support]] ([[Ownership and Assurance Roles]]).
