**Data retention** defines how long records (including logs, extracts, and reporting datasets) are kept and how they are disposed. For audit trails, retention must cover investigation needs and the [[Audit Period]]. Unknown or too-short retention reduces [[Evidence Reliability]] ([[Audit Logging]]).

Align retention with policy and [[Criteria]]; document legal/privacy constraints. Distinct from [[Data Suppression]] (disclosure control).
