**Exception report review** is detective monitoring of exception/override/reject reports **after** the fact—distinct from [[Manual Override Approval]] at time of action and from real-time [[Monitoring and Alerting]].

OE evidence: completed reviews, aging of open exceptions, escalation ([[Manual Control]], [[Operating Effectiveness]]). [[Monitoring and Reporting]] mentions exception reports as governance outputs.
