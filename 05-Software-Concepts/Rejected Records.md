**Rejected records** fail validation or load rules and land in reject/error queues—or drop silently if mishandled. Controls: capture, age, reprocess, and report rejects. Silent drops cause [[Incomplete Audit Logging|completeness]] failures in reporting populations.

EFMS public theme of loading/re-ingestion timeliness is an adjacent completeness lesson—not a CRA-wide reject-queue inventory.
