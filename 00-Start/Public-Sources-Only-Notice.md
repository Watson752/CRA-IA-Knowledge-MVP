# Public Sources Only Notice

Read this notice before using any note in this vault for learning, analysis, or future retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

## Scope of information

This knowledge vault is built **only from publicly accessible information** published by the Government of Canada, the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), and other official public publishers where noted in the [[99-Sources/CRA-Public-Source-Register]]. It does **not** include employment-only information, internal systems documentation, working papers, or any material that is not verifiable from public official sources.

## Not an official CRA product

This vault is a **personal learning and MVP demonstration**. It is **not** an official CRA product, **not** produced by the CRA, and **not endorsed** by the CRA or any other government body. Naming and structure are for navigation convenience only.

## Interpretations and derived content

Summaries, maps, learning paths, bridge notes, and cross-references marked as derived analysis or general professional knowledge reflect the **MVP author's interpretations** for study purposes. They must not be treated as CRA policy, management decisions, or audit conclusions.

Where a note cites an official report or web page, the **original published source remains authoritative**. If this vault and an official source disagree, follow the official source.

## Historical vs current

Published internal audit and evaluation reports describe conditions **as of their audit period**. Findings, recommendations, and management action plans may have changed since publication. **Do not assume** that historical findings are still open, unresolved, or representative of current control effectiveness unless confirmed in a newer official source.

## Privacy and taxpayer data

This vault contains **no taxpayer-level data**, no case-specific compliance information, and no protected information. Only aggregate public statistics and officially published audit narratives appear here, within the limits of what those sources publish.

## AI and retrieval use

Notes may be used to ground future RAG systems only under the rules in [[15-Governance/Public-Source-RAG-Grounding]] and the classification model in [[15-Governance/Content-Classification-Model]]. Synthetic demonstrations (Class D) must never be presented as evidence of CRA facts or audit results.

## Questions and corrections

If you rely on this vault for work decisions, verify every material claim against the linked official URL and publication date in the source register.
