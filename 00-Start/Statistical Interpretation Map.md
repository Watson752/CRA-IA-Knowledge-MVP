# Statistical Interpretation Map

## Detectability vs importance

```text
Analytic result
→ [[Statistical Significance]] / [[Confidence Interval]] / [[Effect Size]]
→ [[Operational Significance]] / [[Materiality]]
→ [[Professional Judgment]] + [[Criteria]]
→ [[Finding]] / [[Audit Conclusion]]
```

Rules taught in this vault:

- No universal significance level as CRA policy
- Statistical significance ≠ operational importance
- Non-significance ≠ proof of no risk ([[Rare High-Impact Events]])
- Large-N: tiny effects may be detectable yet immaterial
