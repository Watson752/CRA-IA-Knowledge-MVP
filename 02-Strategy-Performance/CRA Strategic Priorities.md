# CRA Strategic Priorities

**Three** priorities for **2026–27** from the CRA Departmental Plan.

## A — Preserve the integrity of the tax and benefit system

| Sub-priority | Theme note |
|--------------|------------|
| **A1** | [[Cyber and Data Security]] (cyber, data security, fraud) |
| **A2** | [[Compliance Assurance]] |
| **A3** | [[Tax Debt Collection]] |

## B — Enhance operational efficiency

| Sub-priority | Theme note |
|--------------|------------|
| **B1** | Quality services / people first ([[Digital Service Delivery]]) |
| **B2** | Improve business processes ([[Operational Efficiency]]) |
| **B3** | Digital tools and data-driven approaches ([[Digital and Data-Driven Transformation]]) |

## C — High-performing workforce

| Sub-priority | Theme note |
|--------------|------------|
| **C1** | Digital and data skills ([[CRA Workforce Capability]]) |
| **C2** | Core values ([[CRA Vision Mission and Values]]) |

These priorities apply to the **2026–27** planning period; later plans may differ.
