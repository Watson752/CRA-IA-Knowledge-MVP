# CRA Core Responsibilities

The Departmental Plan **2026–27** identifies **two** core responsibilities.

## Tax

Supports [[Tax Administration]], [[Compliance Assurance]], and [[Tax Debt Collection]] themes in the plan.

**Planned (FY 2026–27, departmental plan):**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|--------|
| Planned spending | $4,572,025,864 |
| Planned human resources | 40,186 FTE |

**Actual (FY 2024–25, departmental results report):**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|--------|
| Actual spending (tax core responsibility) | $4,767,625,053 |

## Benefits

Supports [[Benefits Administration]]; the plan notes administration spanning **210** services and benefits programs.

**Planned (FY 2026–27, departmental plan):**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|--------|
| Planned spending | $659,834,747 |
| Planned human resources | 1,556 FTE |

**Actual (FY 2024–25, departmental results report):**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|--------|
| Actual spending (benefits core responsibility) | $16,182,472,509 |

Benefits **actual spending** includes **statutory benefit payments** and is much larger than tax administration spending; interpret only with DRR labelling and footnotes.

## Agency total (planned 2026–27)

**Planned** total spending **$6,273,543,680** (including internal services) and **49,498** planned FTEs—departmental plan **2026–27**.
