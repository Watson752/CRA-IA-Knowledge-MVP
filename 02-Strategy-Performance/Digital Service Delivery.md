# Digital Service Delivery

Priority **B** (“Enhance operational efficiency”) includes **B1** focused on **quality services**, aligned with the vision of administration that is trusted, fair, and helpful by **putting people first** ([[CRA Vision Mission and Values]], [[CRA Strategic Priorities]]).
