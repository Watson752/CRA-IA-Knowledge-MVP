# CRA Mandate

The CRA’s published **mission** states what the agency is charged to do on behalf of governments in Canada.

## Mission (Departmental Plan 2026–27)

The CRA will **administer tax, benefits, and related programs**, and **ensure compliance** on behalf of governments across Canada, thereby **contributing to the ongoing economic and social well-being of Canadians**.

## Relationship to responsibilities and governance

- Two **core responsibilities** are identified publicly: **Tax** and **Benefits** ([[CRA Core Responsibilities]]).
- Administration and enforcement are carried out under the [[Minister of National Revenue]] with day-to-day responsibility on the [[Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer]]; the [[Board of Management]] oversees organization and administration but not tax law enforcement.
