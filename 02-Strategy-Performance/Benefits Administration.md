# Benefits Administration

**Benefits** is one of two **core responsibilities** ([[CRA Core Responsibilities]]). The mission includes administering **benefits and related programs** ([[CRA Mandate]]).

## Scale (public plan text)

The Departmental Plan **2026–27** references **210** services and benefits programs in the benefits administration context.

## Public financial figures

**Planned (FY 2026–27, departmental plan):** benefits planned spending **$659,834,747**; planned human resources **1,556** FTE.

**Actual (FY 2024–25, departmental results report):** benefits core responsibility actual spending **$16,182,472,509**.

Benefits **actual spending** reflects **statutory benefit payments** and other benefits-related expenditures reported in the results framework—it is **not** directly comparable to tax administration spending without reading how the CRA labels and footnotes these amounts.

## Strategic links

Priority **A** (integrity) and service themes under **B1** ([[CRA Strategic Priorities]], [[Digital Service Delivery]]) apply agency-wide to benefits as well as tax.
