# Compliance Assurance

**A2** under priority **A** (“Preserve the integrity of the tax and benefit system”) focuses on **assuring compliance** ([[CRA Strategic Priorities]]).

## Public mandate link

CRA’s mission includes **ensuring compliance** on behalf of governments across Canada ([[CRA Mandate]]), within the **Tax** core responsibility ([[Tax Administration]]).

## Organizational link

[[02-Organization/Branches/Compliance Programs Branch|CPB]] is a named HQ program branch; detailed compliance strategies are in the departmental plan, not reproduced in full here.
