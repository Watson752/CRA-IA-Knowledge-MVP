# CRA Performance Measurement

Public performance reporting for the CRA uses the **departmental plan** (forward-looking **planned** resources) and the **departmental results report** (**actual** results for a completed fiscal year).

## Planned resources — FY 2026–27 (Departmental Plan)

| Indicator | Value | Label |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Total planned spending (incl. internal services) | $6,273,543,680 | **Planned** 2026–27 |
| Total planned FTEs | 49,498 | **Planned** 2026–27 |
| Tax planned spending | $4,572,025,864 | **Planned** 2026–27 |
| Tax planned FTEs | 40,186 | **Planned** 2026–27 |
| Benefits planned spending | $659,834,747 | **Planned** 2026–27 |
| Benefits planned FTEs | 1,556 | **Planned** 2026–27 |

## Actual spending — FY 2024–25 (Departmental Results Report)

| Indicator | Value | Label |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Tax core responsibility spending | $4,767,625,053 | **Actual** 2024–25 |
| Benefits core responsibility spending | $16,182,472,509 | **Actual** 2024–25 |

Benefits actual spending includes **statutory benefit payments**; do not treat it as “administration cost” without reading CRA reporting definitions.

## Internal audit and evaluation

The IA landing page states audits are **risk-based** and focus on risk management, management control framework, and information for decision-making and reporting; results are posted for **public accountability** ([[Internal Audit and Program Evaluation]]).

## Governance

[[Board of Management]] develops/oversees the **Corporate Business Plan**; [[CRA Departmental Results Framework]] situates plan and results documents.
