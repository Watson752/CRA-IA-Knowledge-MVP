# Operational Efficiency

Priority **B** is to **enhance operational efficiency** ([[CRA Strategic Priorities]]), with sub-priorities:

- **B1** — quality services ([[Digital Service Delivery]])
- **B2** — **improve business processes**
- **B3** — digital tools and data-driven approaches ([[Digital and Data-Driven Transformation]])

## Planned expense reduction (departmental plan)

**Planned** Consolidated Expense Reduction (CER) amounts:

| Fiscal year | Planned CER |
|-------------|-------------|
| 2026–27 | $90,400,446 |
| 2027–28 | $131,714,191 |
| 2028–29 | $187,082,803 |

All figures are **planned** in the **2026–27** departmental plan, not actual savings realized.
