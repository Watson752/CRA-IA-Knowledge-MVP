# CRA Departmental Results Framework

The CRA publishes a **Departmental Plan** and **Departmental Results Report** that together describe expected and achieved results, resources, and priorities in line with federal reporting practice.

## Elements captured in this vault (from provided sources)

### Direction

- [[CRA Vision Mission and Values]]
- [[CRA Mandate]]
- [[CRA Core Responsibilities]] — **Tax** and **Benefits**
- [[CRA Strategic Priorities]] — three priorities for **2026–27** (A, B, C)

### Resources and results

- **Planned** spending and FTEs for **2026–27** in [[CRA Performance Measurement]] and [[CRA Core Responsibilities]]
- **Actual** tax and benefits spending for **2024–25** in the departmental results report (see [[CRA Performance Measurement]])

### Departmental Results Framework (DRF)

The Departmental Plan **2026–27** presents results using the **Departmental Results Framework**. For the planning period, the framework includes **12 departmental result indicators**: **10** under the **Tax** core responsibility and **2** under **Benefits**. Indicator definitions, targets, and planned results appear in the plan tables; **actual** results for the prior year appear in the **2024–25** departmental results report.

Always label **planned** vs **actual** and the **fiscal year** when citing an indicator value.

### Governance linkage

[[Board of Management]] supports world-class administration and **develops the Corporate Business Plan**; the Commissioner manages day-to-day delivery ([[Commissioner and Chief Executive Officer]]).

### Assurance

[[Internal Audit and Program Evaluation]] provides independent assurance aligned with **International Standards for the Professional Practice of Internal Auditing**; audits are risk-based (IA landing page).
