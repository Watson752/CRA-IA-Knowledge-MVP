# CRA Workforce Capability

Priority **C** is a **high-performing workforce** ([[CRA Strategic Priorities]]).

## Sub-priorities

- **C1** — **Digital and data skills** (supports [[Digital and Data-Driven Transformation]])
- **C2** — **Core values** ([[CRA Vision Mission and Values]]: Integrity, Professionalism, Respect, Collaboration)

## Planned workforce scale (agency-wide)

**Planned (FY 2026–27, departmental plan):** **49,498** FTEs total (including internal services).

By core responsibility (**planned 2026–27**): tax **40,186** FTE; benefits **1,556** FTE ([[CRA Core Responsibilities]]).

These are **planned** figures, not **actual** 2024–25 results.
