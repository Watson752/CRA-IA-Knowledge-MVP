**Sample selection** designs which items from a population are tested. Manage [[Sampling Risk]] via random/stratified designs ([[Stratified Sampling]]), size, and coverage across the [[Audit Period]] / [[Control Frequency]]. Non-representative convenience samples increase risk.

For automated controls, consider stratifying by rule version, period slice, and high-risk types.
