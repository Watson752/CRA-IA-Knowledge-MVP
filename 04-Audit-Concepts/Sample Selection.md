**Sample selection** designs which items from the [[Sampling Frame]] are tested. **First** reconcile the frame to the [[Intended Population]] ([[Population Completeness]], [[Data Reconciliation]])—or document a scope limitation—then choose a method:

| Method | Selection basis | Representativeness | Population-wide [[Statistical Extrapolation]] |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Random Sampling]] | Known chance within frame | Designed for frame | May support when design allows |
| [[Stratified Sampling]] | Partition; sample within strata | Improves coverage of segments | May support within design bounds |
| [[Judgmental Sampling]] / [[Risk-Based Selection]] | Deliberate risk/material picks | Not statistical | **Normally unsupported**—item/scoped conclusions only |

Manage [[Sampling Risk]] via design, size, and coverage across the [[Audit Period]] / [[Control Frequency]]. Non-representative convenience samples increase risk. There is **no** universal sample-size formula in this vault; apply [[Materiality]] and [[Risk Assessment]]. For automated controls, consider stratifying by rule version, period slice, and high-risk types. Prefer [[Full-Population Analysis]] when data allow and residual data risks are acceptable.
