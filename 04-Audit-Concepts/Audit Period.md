The **audit period** (period under review) is the time range of subject-matter activity the engagement covers. It differs from the examination/fieldwork dates and from the report date.

State the audit period when interpreting [[Finding]]s. Historical public findings describe conditions in that period—not automatically current conditions. See [[Interpreting Historical Public Audit Findings]].
