An **automated control** is performed by a system (validations, detection rules, automated workflows). Design tests examine logic and configuration; OE tests examine whether the rule remained enabled and operated over the period. Reliance on automated controls typically requires confidence in ITGCs—access and change management ([[IT Controls]], [[Tool Deployment]], [[System-Generated Evidence]]).
