**Audit planning** is the work that turns an assurance need into an executable engagement: clarify the [[Audit Objective]], draft [[Scope]] (including exclusions and [[Audit Period]]), select [[Criteria]], assess [[Risk Assessment|engagement risk]], and design [[Methodology]] / [[Control Testing]] procedures so that [[Evidence]] will be sufficient and appropriate.

Planning is iterative. [[Risk Assessment]] and preliminary inquiries may refine scope before fieldwork. Material changes should be documented and communicated to the [[Audit Client]].

**Content class:** general professional knowledge. Individual CRA reports may describe planning approvals (e.g., Assignment Planning Memorandum) as **official case-specific** facts when stated.
