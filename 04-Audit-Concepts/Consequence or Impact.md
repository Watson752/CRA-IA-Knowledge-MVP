**Consequence or impact** (often called **effect** in finding structures) is the actual or potential harm, exposure, or residual [[Risk]] arising from the gap between condition and [[Criteria]]. It helps prioritize [[Recommendation]]s and [[Management Action Plan]] items.

Consequence is not the same as root cause ([[Root Cause Analysis]]) and not the same as the recommendation.
