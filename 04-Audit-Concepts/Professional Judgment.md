**Professional judgment** is the application of relevant knowledge and experience to decide scope emphasis, evidence sufficiency, finding elevation/aggregation, wording, and whether cause is supported. Judgment does not replace [[Evidence]] or [[Criteria]]; it operates within them and should be documentable.

When interpreting analytics, weigh [[Statistical Significance]], [[Effect Size]], [[Confidence Interval]]s, [[Materiality]], and [[Operational Significance]] together—detectability alone does not decide a [[Finding]]. Distinct from [[Judgmental Sampling]] (a selection method).
