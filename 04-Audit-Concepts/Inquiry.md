**Inquiry** asks knowledgeable persons about processes, controls, or events. Useful for understanding and corroboration, but **inquiry alone does not prove [[Operating Effectiveness]]** for a period—obtain supporting [[Evidence]] ([[Inspection]], [[Reperformance]], logs, etc.).
