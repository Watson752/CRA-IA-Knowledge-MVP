**Operating effectiveness** asks whether the [[Control]] **actually performed** as required:

- by the appropriate person or system;
- at the required [[Control Frequency]];
- during the relevant [[Audit Period]];
- with sufficient [[Evidence]];
- consistently enough to support the conclusion.

**Inquiry alone does not demonstrate operating effectiveness** for the period—corroborate ([[Evidence]], [[Inquiry]]). One successful instance does not prove the whole period ([[Sampling Risk]]). For [[Automated Control]]s, also consider ITGCs (access, change management) under [[IT Controls]].
