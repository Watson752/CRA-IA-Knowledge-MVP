**Operating effectiveness** asks whether the [[Control]] **actually performed** as required:

- by the appropriate person or system;
- at the required [[Control Frequency]];
- during the relevant [[Audit Period]];
- with sufficient [[Evidence]];
- consistently enough to support the conclusion.

**Inquiry alone does not demonstrate operating effectiveness** for the period—corroborate ([[Evidence]], [[Inquiry]]). One successful instance does not prove the whole period ([[Sampling Risk]], [[Control Frequency]]). Pre-production testing does not prove production OE for the period ([[Control Implementation]]).

For [[Automated Control]]s: confirm production enablement across the period, inspect in-period [[Change Management|changes]], use [[Audit Logging]] / transactions / [[Exception Testing]], [[Reperformance]] or [[Full-Population Analysis]] as appropriate, and consider ITGCs (access, change) under [[IT Controls]]. Include exception/override paths ([[Manual Overrides]]) and outcome errors ([[False Positives]], [[False Negatives]]).
