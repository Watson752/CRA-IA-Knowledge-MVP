A **management action plan** (MAP) details how management will implement responses to audit [[Recommendation]]s. It typically lists actions, owners, resources, milestones, and target completion dates. MAPs translate [[Management Response]] commitments into trackable work suitable for project management and [[Follow-up]] verification.

Strong action plans align with [[Control Ownership]], address underlying causes of [[Finding]]s, and define how success will be measured (KPIs, control tests, or reduced exception rates). For IT-related issues, plans may reference [[Tool Deployment]], configuration changes, or [[Monitoring and Reporting]] enhancements.

Auditors may review draft MAPs for completeness but do not own execution. Governance bodies sometimes monitor overdue MAP items. When dates slip, management should document revised timelines and risk acceptance decisions.

In multi-year remediation, plans may phase quick wins and structural changes. Linkage to [[Risk Management]] and [[Continuous Improvement]] helps prioritize across audit cycles.
