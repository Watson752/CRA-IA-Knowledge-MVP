# Substantive Testing

**Content class:** general professional knowledge. This note is not a CRA audit manual, prescribed sampling method, or statement about any current CRA control.

Substantive testing obtains evidence directly about whether the subject matter meets the applicable [[Criteria]]. Depending on the objective, it can include inspecting records, testing transactions, reperforming calculations, or analysing a complete population. It is distinct from testing whether a control was designed or operated effectively, although both may contribute to an [[Audit Conclusion]].

The appropriate population, selection method, period, and evidence reliability must be documented. [[Population Completeness]] and [[Sampling Risk]] affect how far a result can be generalized; [[Full-Population Analysis]] reduces sampling risk but does not by itself establish data validity.
