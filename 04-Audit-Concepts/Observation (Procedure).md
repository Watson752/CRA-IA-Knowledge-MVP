**Observation** as a testing **procedure** means the auditor watches a control or process being performed. It often supports understanding of [[Design Effectiveness]] or provides a point-in-time signal. Observation alone rarely proves [[Operating Effectiveness]] for an entire [[Audit Period]].

Do not confuse with [[Audit Observation]] (a pre-finding issue note).
