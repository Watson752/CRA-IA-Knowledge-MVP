**Content class:** general professional / teaching term **audit client** (or auditee). **Official case-specific** labels in CRA public reports often use **OPI**, management response owner, or **MAP owner** instead of the phrase “audit client.”

The **audit client** is the management party responsible for the subject under review: it provides access and [[Evidence]], clarifies process facts, receives [[Finding]]s, and submits [[Management Response]] / [[Management Action Plan]] commitments. The client **cooperates** with internal audit; it is **not** the auditor and does not perform independent assurance ([[Internal Audit Independence]]).

In CRA vault usage, **Office of Primary Interest (OPI)** means the business area primarily responsible for the audited subject ([[CRA-Acronym-Dictionary]]). OPI aligns with audit-client responsibility for scope and accountability but may not name every interviewed or co-responsible partner. [[Scope]] defines what is in the engagement; OPI identifies who management designates as primary accountable party for that scope in report language.

Other branches (e.g., [[Information Technology Branch|ITB]] as technical support) may be in scope, interviewed, or named in action plans without being OPI/MAP lead— see [[Ownership and Assurance Roles]] BI and EFMS examples.
