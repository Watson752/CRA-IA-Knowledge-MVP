**Document review** inspects policies, procedures, specs, and retained records. Closely related to [[Inspection]]; useful for design evidence. Documentation alone does not prove [[Operating Effectiveness]] ([[Control]], [[Design Effectiveness]]).

EFMS published methodology emphasized documentation review—do not equate that with production configuration disclosure or full OE reperformance.
