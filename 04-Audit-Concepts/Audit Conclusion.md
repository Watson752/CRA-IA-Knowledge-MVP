An **audit conclusion** is the engagement-level statement of what was determined relative to the [[Audit Objective]], [[Scope]], and [[Criteria]]. It differs from individual [[Finding]]s and from [[Recommendation]]s.

Conclusion **strength** must match evidence strength ([[How Statistical Limitations Affect Audit Conclusions]], [[Evidence Reliability]]). When [[Population Completeness]] cannot be established, narrow wording to the [[Retrieved Population]], qualify, or use directional language—do not imply coverage of the full [[Intended Population]].

[[Reasonable Assurance]] is high but not absolute; sampling, data, and system limits remain.
