**Content class:** general professional concept for independence; **official public CRA fact** that [[Audit, Evaluation, and Risk Branch|AERB]] provides independent assurance and evaluation (branch mandate); **case-specific** where a report applies [[Three Lines Model]] language (e.g., cyber case third line).

**Internal audit independence** means the assurance function can perform work and report without undue influence from the activities it reviews. Independence supports objective [[Finding]]s, candid [[Recommendation]]s, and credible follow-up— it does **not** transfer ownership of business processes, systems, data, or corrective action to auditors.

At CRA, AERB publishes via [[Internal Audit and Program Evaluation]]; that publishing role is not the same as being MAP owner. Auditors may review draft [[Management Action Plan]]s for completeness but **do not own execution** ([[Management Action Plan]]). Management owns [[Management Response]] and remediation ([[Management Action Plan Owner]]).

The [[Three Lines Model]] places internal audit in the **third line**: assurance distinct from first-line control operation and second-line monitoring/challenge. TBS internal audit policy expectations for CAE independence and formal management responses appear in [[99-Sources/source-notes/SRC-TBS-Policy-Internal-Audit]].

Independence is preserved when auditors name reasonable MAPs— judging plans is assurance; implementing them remains management accountability.
