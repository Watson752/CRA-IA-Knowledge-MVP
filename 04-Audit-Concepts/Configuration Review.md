**Configuration review** inspects runtime [[System Configuration]] (and related rule objects) against approved design—for design capability and for production enablement. Alone, it does not prove full-period [[Operating Effectiveness]] without change history and operation evidence.

Named as an IT-audit method theme in [[Methodology]].
