**Risk assessment** prioritizes where limited audit resources focus. Distinguish:

1. **Enterprise / annual planning risk assessment** — builds the audit plan from organizational [[Risk]] profiles ([[Risk Management]]).
2. **Engagement-level risk assessment** — refines [[Scope]], [[Methodology]], and testing depth for one engagement.

Risk assessment informs—but does not replace—[[Criteria]] or [[Evidence]]. Documentation of risk assessment is not proof that residual risk is acceptable.
