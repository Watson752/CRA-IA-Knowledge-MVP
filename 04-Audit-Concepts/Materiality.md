**Materiality** is the threshold of importance—quantitative or qualitative—at which a misstatement, control gap, or analytical difference could influence the decisions of intended users or the elevation of a [[Finding]]. It connects [[Risk Assessment]], [[Consequence or Impact]], and [[Operational Significance]].

There is **no** universal numeric materiality percentage in this vault and none is asserted as CRA policy. Apply [[Professional Judgment]] relative to the [[Audit Objective]] and [[Criteria]].

Uses: sample design focus; which [[Outlier Analysis|outliers]]/trends to investigate deeply; whether a statistically detectable difference warrants reporting ([[Statistical Significance]] ≠ automatic materiality).
