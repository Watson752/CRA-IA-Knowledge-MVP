An **audit observation** is a noted condition from fieldwork that has **not** (yet) been elevated to a formal [[Finding]]. Immaterial exceptions or isolated errors may remain observations communicated to management without public-report treatment ([[Finding]]).

**Disambiguation:** this note is **not** the testing procedure of watching a process. For the procedure, see [[Observation (Procedure)]].

```text
Evidence → Audit Observation → (assessed against Criteria) → may become Finding
```
