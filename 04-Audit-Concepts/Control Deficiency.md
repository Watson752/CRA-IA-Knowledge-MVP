A **control deficiency** (control weakness) is a design gap, operating failure, or missing [[Control]] relative to the [[Risk]] and [[Criteria]]. Weak or missing controls increase exposure and **may** support a [[Finding]] when paired with [[Evidence]] of adverse conditions—but **not every** deficiency is reportable ([[Risk]], [[Finding]]).

Distinguish from [[Audit Observation]] (noted condition not yet elevated) and from [[Finding]] (significant reported issue).
