**Design effectiveness** asks: if the [[Control]] is performed as intended, is it **capable** of addressing the identified [[Risk]] / [[Control Objective]]?

Typical design work: understand the risk; inspect policies/configs; [[Walkthrough]]; assess logic of automated rules ([[Automated Control]], [[IT Controls]]). A walkthrough that confirms understanding of design is **not** by itself [[Operating Effectiveness]] testing for the whole [[Audit Period]].

Documentation of a control’s design is not proof it operated.
