**Management response** is management’s formal reaction to audit [[Finding]]s and [[Recommendation]]s, typically included in or appended to the audit report. Responses may agree, partially agree, or disagree, and should explain rationale, planned actions, target dates, and responsible officials. They demonstrate accountability and create a baseline for [[Follow-up]].

Effective responses address root cause, not only immediate correction. They may accept risk where remediation is disproportionate, subject to governance approval and documented rationale. Disagreements should be factual and specific so readers can assess whether the audit conclusion or management position is more persuasive.

In public sector practice, responses are often reviewed by senior management and may be published alongside audit reports for transparency. The response does not replace a [[Management Action Plan]] with detailed milestones, but it should commit to one where complex remediation is required.

Auditors evaluate whether responses are responsive; inadequate responses may elevate follow-up priority or governance escalation paths described in the internal audit charter.
