**Reasonable assurance** is a high but not absolute level of confidence that engagement conclusions are appropriate. Internal audit engagements typically seek reasonable assurance, not certainty. Limitations of [[Scope]], [[Evidence]], sampling, and systems mean findings do not “guarantee” that risks are managed ([[Internal Audit Independence]], [[Three Lines Model]]).
