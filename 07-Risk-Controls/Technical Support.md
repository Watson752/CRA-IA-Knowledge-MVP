**Content class:** general professional concept for the role type; **official public CRA fact** that [[Information Technology Branch|ITB]] develops, operates, maintains, and evolves CRA IT (branch mandate on org baseline).

**Technical support** means building, operating, evolving, and securing the technology that enables business processes— platforms, integrations, service delivery, and often shared infrastructure. It is **not** the same as [[Business Process Owner|business-process ownership]]: enabling a capability does not automatically make IT accountable for the business outcome or for every MAP lead role in a public audit.

Interns and analysts should avoid statements such as “IT owns the program” when cases only show ITB as **service delivery**, co-chair, co-maintainer, or named collaborator ([[Internal Audit - Oversight Use and Continuous Improvement of Business Intelligence]]: ITB BI service delivery while SIIB holds BI responsibility).

Technical support partners with process and [[System Owner|system accountability]] through governance forums, change management, and [[Control Ownership]] splits (business vs technical custodians). [[Three Lines Model]] first-line operators may include both business units and IT operations depending on the control.
