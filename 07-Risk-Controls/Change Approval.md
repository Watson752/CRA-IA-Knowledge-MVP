**Content class:** general professional role/control concept—not a CRA org-chart title.

**Change approval** authorizes a proposed change (business intent / risk acceptance) **before** implementation or release. The change approver role is distinct from the technical implementer and from [[Deployment Approval]] go-live authority. Do not invent CRA CAB structures not present in public sources.
