**Content class:** general professional concept ([[Ownership and Assurance Roles]] for how this fits CRA onboarding).

**Control ownership** assigns named individuals or roles accountability for designing, operating, and monitoring specific [[Control]]s— not merely executing tasks but ensuring controls remain fit for purpose as [[Risk]] and systems change. Owners document control activities, perform self-assessments, and remediate gaps.

Without ownership, controls decay, access reviews lapse, and audit [[Recommendation]]s lack sustained fixes. Ownership maps to [[Roles and Responsibilities]] and integrates with [[Risk Management]] registers that list controls mitigating each risk.

IT and security controls often split ownership between business process owners and technical custodians; interfaces must be clear to avoid gaps. [[Management Action Plan]] items should name owners with sufficient authority.

Auditors test whether stated owners understand their controls and evidence operation— a common source of [[Finding]]s when documentation exists but practice differs.
