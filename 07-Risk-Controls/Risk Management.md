**Risk management** is the coordinated activity to identify, assess, treat, monitor, and communicate [[Risk]] relative to objectives. Enterprise risk management integrates risks across strategies, operations, reporting, and compliance. It informs prioritization of [[Control]]s, investments, and audit plans.

Standard process: context setting, identification, analysis, evaluation, treatment (avoid, reduce, share, accept), and monitoring. Risk appetite and tolerance statements translate board intent into operational limits.

The [[Three Lines Model]] places risk ownership in the first line, frameworks and challenge in the second, and independent assurance in internal audit. [[Risk Algorithms]] may automate parts of identification or prioritization with distinct governance needs.

Auditors rely on risk registers and assessments but validate with [[Evidence]]— not assuming documentation equals effective management.
