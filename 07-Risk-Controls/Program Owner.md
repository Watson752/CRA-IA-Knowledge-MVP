**Content class:** general professional concept for “program owner”; **official public CRA facts** for how [[CRA-Program-Branches]] vs [[CRA-Corporate-Branches]] are described on the org baseline; **official case-specific** when a published audit names an OPI or MAP owner for that program.

A **program owner** (in governance language) is accountable for a program’s objectives, performance, and sustained compliance with policy— often at headquarters for CRA **program branches** that provide technical and policy support for tax administration programs. Regional delivery through [[CRA-Regions]] can involve the same program without every region being the “owner” in audit report language.

Public audits frequently identify the **Office of Primary Interest (OPI)** or **MAP owner** for the audited subject ([[Audit Client]], [[CRA-Acronym-Dictionary]]). Examples in this vault (each tied to its case, not generalized into reporting lines):

- Collections inventory — [[Collections and Verification Branch|CVB]] as OPI/MAP pattern in ARNI case materials
- Charities audit process — program branch named in charities case org section
- Business intelligence — [[Service, Innovation, and Integration Branch|SIIB]] responsibility and MAP lead in BI case

Program ownership is related to but not identical to [[Business Process Owner]]: a program may span several processes; a process may cut across programs with shared [[Technical Support]].
