**Content class:** general professional / teaching catalogue—not a claim that CRA eligibility programs currently exhibit these weaknesses.

**Eligibility decision risks** when automated rules and human discretion interact include: incorrect decisions ([[Incorrect Automated Decisions]]); inconsistent treatment of similar cases; policy non-alignment; unsupported discretion; delayed processing; financial or service impacts; reputational consequences. Connect software control gaps (weak approval, logging, review) to these business outcomes when scoping inquiry—not as established findings.
