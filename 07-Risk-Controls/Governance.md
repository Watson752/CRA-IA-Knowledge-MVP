**Governance** is the system by which organizations are directed and controlled— defining authority, accountability, oversight, and ethical expectations. It includes boards or equivalent bodies, executive leadership, policies, and assurance functions. Effective governance aligns strategy, [[Risk Management]], and resource use with [[Enterprise Objectives]] and stakeholder interests.

Key themes: clear [[Roles and Responsibilities]], transparency, control over financial and non-financial reporting, and independence of internal audit ([[Three Lines Model]]). [[Control Ownership]] must be embedded in management, not delegated entirely to audit.

Public sector governance adds ministers, legislation, access to information, and value-for-money expectations. Auditors evaluate governance processes as part of many engagements— tone at the top, committee effectiveness, and issue escalation.

Governance connects to [[Data Governance]] and [[Information and Data Strategy]] for data-driven organizations.
